## 1. [2017] Elisa — My perfect personal movie experience - Build the coolest enriched video experience app

- **年份 (Year)**: 2017
- **合作方 (Partner)**: Elisa
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Entertainment
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: My perfect personal movie experience - Build the coolest enriched video experience app

### 挑战描述 (Description)

Customers love to enjoy great movies from online video streaming services. However, finding the interesting thing to watch is not always simple or fun - that is why most people get their movie recommendations directly from friends. Googling around the internet to read reviews and top-lists is time consuming. There is a mass of information available about a movie but not in a convenient or enjoyable way. And once you have seen the great movie, you also might have a tempting need to know more about it or know what other people have said about it. Google or any social media is one way to find some of that information, but we think that it is not enough.\nWe challenge you to build a spectacular customer experience for finding interesting movies that match with my interests and desires right now or in the future, via social discussions and recommendations, and other information about the movie, such as the story, actors or making of the movie, from various sources in the internet. The experience should combine all relevant and valuable information about a movie or series in a cool and exciting way, so that it helps the user to make choices and dig deeper into the movie. You can enrich the discovery experience and user’s decision-making with for example social media comments, video clips, pictures, interviews, events, background information, and peer reviews. Think about deciding if you are going to watch a movie: What would you like to know about it? How should this information be provided to you inside the app so that it really adds value to you? \nYour solution should address one or more of the following user problems:\na)        How to find movies I like?\nb)        How to plan my watching?\nc)        How to discover what others are saying and take part in discussions?\nAs an outcome, we would like this customer frustration to be addressed: “As a user, I would like to easily explore and watch something that matches with my situation, mood and desires. I would like to know everything there is about the movie in a cool and exciting way. This helps me to decide if I am going to watch the movie or not. I can also find interesting additional information about the movie and see what other people are thinking about it. I can do all this in one application.”

### 挑战洞察 (Challenge Insights)

We will provide you with:\n1.        A skeleton of video content browsing and playback mobile application\n2.        A catalogue of movie titles with basic metadata (such as title name, title length, actors, genre, trailers, pictures, popularity) as well as the APIs to access this content\n3.        List of valuable external information sources and APIs (social media, sites)\n4.        On-site support and experts\n5.        Smart TVs, VR glasses, game consoles, mobile devices

### 评审标准 (Judging Criteria)

The user experience in the app with the enriched and visual movie related information is the key element here. Genuine ideas and enabling easy decision-making for the user are winning elements.  We are especially interested in finding solutions that do not just show the enriched data, but build value for the user on top of it.

### 奖项设置 (Prizes)

The prizes for the winning teams will be announced later.

### 可用资源 (Resources)

We will not provide you data about the user. So you will need to ask or find the data you need about the user, her preferences and desires in order to build the best ever movie experience.

### 相关链接 (Links)

You can focus on one of the user problems deeply or solve all three of them (a-c). The app (on any device) also needs to work in real life. You do not need to use all the provided building elements (1-5). Additional ideas can also be presented even when not developed to the app, but you will not win just with great ideas. Solutions that build user value on top of the data is what we are after.

### 关于合作方 (About Company)

Elisa Viihde is all you need to feel entertained and Finland´s most popular multiscreen entertainment service. It serves more than 300 000 customers. Elisa is a telecommunications, ICT and online service company serving 2.3 million consumer, corporate and public administration organisation customers.
