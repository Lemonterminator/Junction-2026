## 1. [2017] Planmeca Oy — Reshaping the future of dental care. - Dive into the world of technology and use it to revolutionize dental care.

- **年份 (Year)**: 2017
- **合作方 (Partner)**: Planmeca Oy
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Health
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Reshaping the future of dental care. - Dive into the world of technology and use it to revolutionize dental care.

### 挑战描述 (Description)

Digital revolution is transforming the entire field of health care and patient care is no longer just taking care of the sick but preventing them from getting sick in the first place. For health care professionals new technology is a way to more efficient, more accurate and more comfortable care. Digital solutions can move the boundaries of health and dental care – the care pathway can start long before the visit to the clinic and go on long after. With advanced 3D imaging technology we can already create a precise digital twin of the patient, which raises a question whether we even need a visit to the dentist any longer?

### 挑战洞察 (Challenge Insights)

To support the innovation process of the teams Planmeca will provide the participants with plenty of information regarding dental care industry and some of our existing solutions. Many of our equipment and solutions will be at the event for the participants to see and try out, including a dental care unit, 3D imaging device, intraoral scanner, and dental software. Participants will also have a chance to see and use real patient data in a form of a 3D virtual patient. Our mentors on site will coach participants and help them with any arising questions.

### 评审标准 (Judging Criteria)

We want to challenge people to think outside the box and expand the ideas of dental care into the digital world. There are two sides to this which we are especially interested in this particular challenge: the concept of a virtual clinic for patients and the virtual patient for clinics. \n\nHack into the patient experience and make it more effortless and less interruptive for the patient. On the other side of this, we want to give the dental care professionals tools for planning and preparing care without the patient being physically present, which improves the patient experience but also increases precision and accuracy of care.

### 奖项设置 (Prizes)

Prizes to be announced.

### 可用资源 (Resources)

### 相关链接 (Links)

Ultimately our mission is to make patient care better and safer by helping the health care professionals to make more accurate diagnoses and give their patients the best possible care. We want participants to embrace this mission, but throw away all limitations and think outside the box while doing so! The following criteria will be used to evaluate the performance and ideas of the teams: Creativity and originality, disruptiveness and feasibility for healthcare.

### 关于合作方 (About Company)

Planmeca Oy is one of the world's leading dental equipment manufacturers. Our products cover everything needed at a modern dental clinic – from digital dental units to CAD/CAM solutions, 2D and 3D imaging devices, and software. Our uncompromising dedication to innovation, collaboration with health care professionals and universities, and strong commitment to R&D are the cornerstones of our success. Our mission is to enable better patient care through revolutionary innovations.

## 7. [2019] CGI & Sitra — Reimagine Mental Health Care

- **年份 (Year)**: 2019
- **合作方 (Partner)**: CGI & Sitra
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Health
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Reimagine Mental Health Care

### 挑战描述 (Description)

The challenge is to choose one or more of the problems and you can use whatever technologies you want to use. Try to find answers to: How and when we could strengthen young people's good mental health and reduce factors that undermine it? \nWho should provide the help and when?  \nHow we can provide the help youngsters need, keep them healthy and in the workforce? \nHow could we provide help in different phases of youth? \nWhat kind of extra tools or services could we provide when young adults enter the workforce?\nHow could different institutions work together to provide seamless services through ecosystems to combat mental health issues? \n\nRemember that the solution should follow the fair data economy principles:\n- data in a digital service or ecosystem are only shared through the individual’s or other data source’s consent.\n- data sharing is made transparent by shared rules. Data is not shared with third parties.\n- The individual has the possibility to track how their data is used and for what purpose.

### 挑战洞察 (Challenge Insights)

Mental disorder is the most significant health problem among the young people in Finland. According to THL, around 20-25 % of our young in Finland will have some kind of mental disorder before they become adults. The most typical mental disorders are anxiety, depression, eating disorder and stress. (Kunttu, Pesonen ja Saari 2017) The symptoms of depression is found among 15 % of young people, anxiety among 10%, 10-15% have repetitive suicidal thoughts and 3-5 % of them have tried to commit a suicide.(Tolmunen, 2019)\n\nIn Finland, the need for mental health care has increased rapidly during the past few years. Many studies propose that the young people do not have more problems than before, but their attitude is different compared to the earlier generations. They are not ashamed and do not have the attitude ‘’I need to survive on my own’’ and thus are more open for help. One of the problem is that we as a society do not start solving these problems early enough.\n\nAccording to researcher Mattila-Holappa’s study, the number of early retirement of young people has been raising year by year for ten years now. Keeping young people in the workforce is also crucial for the economy: a young adult’s disability pension can have direct costs of around 0,5m€ along with other indirect costs.
