# Junction AI Infrastructure Related Awards Audit (2021–2025)

## Scope and evidence rule

This audit concerns public winner records from 2021 through 2025 for challenges currently grouped under `AI Infrastructure` in the silver archive. A challenge brief describes what organizers asked for; it does not establish what a winning team built. Winner claims and implementation details below are reported separately. “No winner record verified” means the sources checked here did not establish the result; it does not mean the award was not given.

## Audit notes for downstream synthesis

- Keep award types distinct: overall event winner, partner challenge winner, and technology challenge winner are separate claims. A project can win more than one, but each needs evidence.
- The public Junction 2025 post confirms 18 challenge winners, 11 tech-challenge winners, and identifies WAVELET as overall winner; it does not list all partner/tech winners in the text retrieved here: [Junction post embedded in LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/zhang-zeyu_looks-like-our-intern-arjun-just-won-junction-activity-7395845983248752641-TaKb).
- In `docs/past/gold/{en,zh}/ai_infrastructure.md`, the 2025 ElevenLabs case has unusually precise performance and implementation claims that are not supported by the winner posts cited above. Treat the winner as supported, but the specific stack/metrics as unverified pending a source-code/demo inspection.
- The 2025 n8n entry appears in the AI Infrastructure gold synthesis, but n8n is an agent-workflow challenge, not one of the 20 challenges listed in the AI Infrastructure silver archive. Its first-place status and detailed performance numbers require direct award/demo/code evidence; a shortened LinkedIn URL alone does not substantiate the architecture or benchmark claims.
- The 2025 WithSecure “Reputation Recon” entry similarly lacks a corresponding challenge in the AI Infrastructure silver inventory. A project repository can substantiate implementation, not a Junction award. A separate participant post establishes a 3rd-place WithSecure result for a different project, CISO Insight, so project identity and placement must be checked before gold uses the award.
