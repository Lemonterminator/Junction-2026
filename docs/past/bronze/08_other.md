## 3. [2020] QOCO — Smooth Travels

- **年份 (Year)**: 2020
- **合作方 (Partner)**: QOCO
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Other
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Smooth Travels

### 挑战描述 (Description)

As airlines are aiming to improve the customer experience through digital solutions, one crucial aspect is often forgotten: what happens to your luggage?\n\nBeing able to follow each step of your mail deliveries has long been a standard convenience, yet when it comes to luggage you’re left with just hoping it makes it to the end destination on time - and if it doesn’t, figuring out where it actually is can be quite tricky.\n\nMillions of pieces of luggage get lost every year, and packing according to airline restrictions can be a pain. At the same time, airlines are tightening the restrictions on how much luggage you can bring in the cabin with you, encouraging flyers to check in their bags instead.\n\nHow could your team help improve the customer experience for flyers traveling with luggage?\n\nWe are looking for new ways to improve the existing baggage operations. Whether it's helping the passenger to pack lighter or track and reduce missing luggage, the solution should make traveling an easier, smoother and more comfortable experience.

### 挑战洞察 (Challenge Insights)

No idea where to start? Here are some ideas of what your solution could do:\nProvide customers with more insight\nHelp airlines track the lost baggage (or even better; help prevent it from being lost in the first place!)\nHelp passengers to pack lighter and according to travel restrictions (packing restricted items is the main reason for having your luggage ransacked)\nHelp the baggage information share (tag > passenger > airline)\nThese are just some examples. Be creative, the sky's the limit!

### 可用资源 (Resources)

QOCO will provide: Luggage API, from which you can get the needed luggage handling data.

### 挑战描述 (Description)

This weekend, we invite you to use the Miro platform to expand on our mission and empower your own audience to create and collaborate in today’s world.\n\nWe’ve been working on some new things for our platform, and for this challenge, you’ll have access to a preview of what’s coming. ## 6. [2022] Keksintösäätiö — Paving the way for tomorrow’s inventions

- **年份 (Year)**: 2022
- **合作方 (Partner)**: Keksintösäätiö
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Other
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Paving the way for tomorrow’s inventions

### 挑战描述 (Description)

Finland is known for the inventiveness, creativity, and pragmatic problem-solving capabilities of the people and society. Finnish inventors rank high in global patent applications and large companies have world-class R&D management and processes.  \\nEntrepreneurs and inventors have a great number of innovative ideas and seeds of inventions. They have, however, great difficulties in making these new ideas come to life and developing their early-stage inventions into marketable, successful innovations.  \\nTo solve the problem, Keksintösäätiö is building a country-wide network of experts from various fields to assess, evaluate, spar, and support entrepreneurs and inventors in their ventures. The Ideajalostamo-program engages these experts and innovation support organizations to form an “umbrella” for delivering enhanced support for target groups. This network requires an online platform to facilitate its work. Keksintösäätiö now wishes to design and build that platform.\\n\\nWe need new innovative out-of-the-box ideas and concepts on how the platform should work. This challenge is a key action towards that goal.\\n\\nhttps://www.keksintosaatio.fi/en/idearefinery/

### 评审标准 (Judging Criteria)

The qualities we are looking for are the EXCELLENCE and the potential IMPACT of the outcomes from the project. As a result of Junction, we hope to have 1. Great ideas and insight into how the platform should be built and what features and services to include 2. Clear implementable concept to try out, demonstrate and take steps forward with.

### 奖项设置 (Prizes)

1st prize: 1200 euro, 2nd prize: 600 euro, A special prize for a high-value out-of-the-box idea is an invitation to the annual IPR Gala.

### 可用资源 (Resources)

Keksintösäätiö is deeply aware of the problems and needs of early-stage inventor-innovators, as well as the needs of innovation support organizations that support entrepreneurs and inventors. We will be at your service to spar and evaluate any ideas arising during the hackathon work.

### 挑战描述 (Description)

People flow is the way how people move in and between place A to place B. Good people flow means people moving smoothly, safely, comfortably, and without waiting. In cruises people flow can be discovered from the passengers’ and crew’s points of view.\\n\\nThink about the service that is visible and non-visible for cruise passengers. You may use your imagination not only with robots but with automation systems, as well.\\n\\nWe encourage you to be creative with your conclusion.
