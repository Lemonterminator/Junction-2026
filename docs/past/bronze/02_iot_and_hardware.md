## 1. [2017] Suunto — Track anything that moves - Build a Movesense based tracking concept for sports, health or something else

- **年份 (Year)**: 2017
- **合作方 (Partner)**: Suunto
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: IoT & Hardware
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Track anything that moves - Build a Movesense based tracking concept for sports, health or something else

### 挑战描述 (Description)

The world is full of things that move – whether it’s an athlete’s body or sports equipment, a machine on a production line, a bridge under traffic, any of us in our daily life, or anything else that we can imagine. Often it would be highly valuable to know how and when these things move – or don’t move. Movesense offers a programmable motion sensor, API’s and software libraries that enable you to quickly build your own sensor functions and mobile applications to track anything that moves. Help the world to stay on the move!

### 挑战洞察 (Challenge Insights)

Participants will have an opportunity to build applications to the Movesense sensor and compatible mobile apps to iOS and Android. Sample apps for the sensor and mobile phone enable raw data collection and show how data can be processed and stored with the sensor and a mobile device. The whole system is based on simple REST API’s. The sensor is programmed with C++. A data connection with IBM Bluemix and Microsoft Azure will be available.\nWhat we’ll bring:\n-        A batch of Movesense sensors, enough for all projects. The sensor you use will be yours to keep after the hack if you submit a project to this challenge.\n-        Programming jigs for fast upload and debugging of the sensor apps\n-        Our Movesense software experts to support your work

### 评审标准 (Judging Criteria)

How can motion tracking help to optimize training of strength, speed, technique, balance, agility, tactics, or any other specific or generic area of sports performance that is not measured today?\nWhat about health and wellbeing? There is much more than daily steps and calories that we need to know to live a healthy live or to recover after a health problem. Can you improve our life quality with motion tracking?\nOr would you rather track something else than human beings? Optimize a production process? Digitalize agriculture? Improve logistics? There are so many moving things in the world. But, when and how to move or not to move? Well, that’s the question.

### 奖项设置 (Prizes)

All teams that submit a Movesense based project can keep the Movesense sensors used in development. As winner prizes, we will hand out the latest Suunto Spartan watch for each team member of the best solutions in three categories: 1. Sports 2. Health and Wellbeing 3. General IoT In addition, teams with commercially viable solutions for sports and daily wellbeing will have an opportunity to talk with Suunto about partnering options.

### 可用资源 (Resources)

Find out more at www.movesense.com.\nhttps://bitbucket.org/suunto/movesense-device-lib (public from early September)\nhttps://bitbucket.org/suunto/movesense-mobile-lib (will be made public soon)

### 相关链接 (Links)

The main criteria are the level of added value provided to the selected use case, functional readiness of the concept, and perceived user experience. If you solve an obvious need or a problem with Movesense in an elegant and easy to use way, your chances of winning are high.

### 关于合作方 (About Company)

Suunto is a Finnish manufacturer of world renowned sports watches, dive computers and precision instruments for the toughest conditions. Our new initiative, Movesense, is an open development environment and a programmable sensor for motion tracking and other sensoring for and beyond sports. With Movesense, you can commercialize your own sensor ideas quickly and cost effectively without having to worry about hardware development and manufacturing.

### 挑战描述 (Description)

Develop a solution or solutions that generate information by joining, analyzing and visualizing open data sets. ## 5. [2018] Ericsson — Saved by IoT

- **年份 (Year)**: 2018
- **合作方 (Partner)**: Ericsson
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: IoT & Hardware
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Saved by IoT

### 挑战描述 (Description)

Imagine a situation where a natural disaster hits your neighborhood, and local communication infrastructure is knocked out. In the middle of the havoc, how can you reach out and tell your family and loved ones that you are still here, alive and kicking? Or indicate that you are hurt, in danger, needing help?\nSmartphones and wearable devices collect lots of data, which can be made available to indicate the wearer’s whereabouts and condition. The sensor data streams can be analyzed and interpreted for example by machine learning solutions in cloud (or any other suitable place), to produce and make available indication of danger, aliveness, etc.\nThis challenge is twofold:\n- Provide (at least) a conceptual plan with evidence of technical feasibility on how to create and deploy distributed narrow band IoT connectivity when local infra is down.\n- Build an application which demonstrates the capability of applicable wearable device to provide sensor data and acting as a gateway towards IoT cloud. Note that the same solution may be used for smaller scale incidents (like car accidents) when the local communication infrastructure is intact.

- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Smart Signage - For Student by Students

### 挑战描述 (Description)

The Aalto University Student Union (AYY) owns about 2600 apartments, which house around 3500 tenants. AYY has been planning to install information screens (smart signage) to the stairwells and hallways of its apartment buildings. At their best, the solutions to this challenge could also improve the experience of communality between the students living in AYY’s housing.

## 9. [2021] Helvar — Brighter Spaces: Intelligent Lighting

- **年份 (Year)**: 2021
- **合作方 (Partner)**: Helvar
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: IoT & Hardware
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Brighter Spaces: Intelligent Lighting

### 挑战描述 (Description)

In this challenge, we ask you to utilise, apply and visualise the sensor data to understand the use of the building and to improve applying the sensor network optimally in the building in the first place. You can select one or all three of the parts of the challenge to work on.\n\nWhere are all the devices?\nWe typically spend a lot of effort on identifying and positioning a large number of sensors on a floorplan. Can we simplify the process, and use the data from a subset of the sensors to automatically place the rest on a floorplan?\n\nWhere is everyone going?\nMotion sensors are useful in determining the amount of activity in a space. We also know what kind of space it is, for example, a hallway or a corridor. Can we identify what are the most used pathways in a building, given the hundreds of thousands of motion events the sensors are generating?\n\nMystery Garage\nThere are moving vehicles and pedestrians in a garage. We have motion and audio sensors. Can we identify what is happening in the garage? Are there vehicles coming in or going out? Are pedestrians walking towards the elevators, or getting in their cars?

### 可用资源 (Resources)

We will provide layouts of the different floorplans containing device locations and time-series of motion and audio events.\n\nDatasets:\nBUILDINGS The participants are given datasets from indoor premises. The data will be completely anonymised. The floorplans will contain devices and their locations. The dataset will be a time series of motion events from all the sensors on the floorplan.\n\nGARAGE The participants are given a training dataset from an indoor garage, collected using motion and audio sensors. \n\nYou are encouraged to use other data sources and feel free to use data from/combine your ideas with other Junction challenges if you see a fit.
