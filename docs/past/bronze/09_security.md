## 1. [2018] Ericsson — Threat Intelligence FTW

- **年份 (Year)**: 2018
- **合作方 (Partner)**: Ericsson
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Security
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Threat Intelligence FTW

### 挑战描述 (Description)

The amount of cyber-attacks is growing all the time. Security analysts spend a lot of their precious time looking for new threats, finding information about the threat actors and to connect the dots manually. Ericsson challenges you to jump into a security analyst’s stinky shoes (pun intended) and create a solution to deal with this almost unbearable task. Can you make a system that will smell the new threats before they reach you, and shed light to the structure and organization of the threat actors? Can you figure out what the internet has to say about this new threat actor you just stumbled upon? Can you combine indicators of compromise for these threats with the human readable information about the related threat actors all available on the internet?\nThere will be three focus areas in the challenge.\n1. Choosing and information retrieval from different sources\n2. Implementation of a data processing solution to filter and combine the relevant issues from retrieved sources\n3. Presenting the resulting information to the security analyst in a valuable way

## 2. [2019] Ericsson — Security Awareness for Kids

- **年份 (Year)**: 2019
- **合作方 (Partner)**: Ericsson
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Security
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Security Awareness for Kids

### 挑战描述 (Description)

Security in the connected world is important for all of us. Ericsson as a global service and solution provider considers improvements on security awareness as a top priority. Security threats are everywhere, and we all are targets. The purpose of this challenge is to strengthen the users’ resilience against these threats through improved awareness.\n\nThere are no strict requirements for the technical level of the solution and therefore this challenge is for you to show off also your skills in design, prototyping and creativity overall. We are looking for the solutions that raise cybersecurity awareness amongst kids.

## 4. [2019] D-Fence — How to make invisible cloud service visible to users?

- **年份 (Year)**: 2019
- **合作方 (Partner)**: D-Fence
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Security
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: How to make invisible cloud service visible to users?

### 挑战描述 (Description)

We at D-Fence make security service which rejects email spam & viruses from reaching the end users. Our clients are very happy with our service, but actually only few may know that they are using it. Usually the client does not have to do anything once the service is enabled, which in itself is great, but at the same time creates the problem of many of the clients not knowing or forgetting that they are in trusted hands.\n\nHow could you help us to make our security services more engaging. Could you help out how we could make the service more visible to the end users? What benefit could it bring both the individual user and the client as a whole?\n\nWhat if the end user could have some kind of “icon”, that could be used for different kind of functions? Like sending samples of received spam, colour signaling the “threat level”.\nNew visual and content outlay for the monthly security report.

### 可用资源 (Resources)

D-fence will provide: Current technology and process how we inform our customers with a monthly security report now.  We can provide a sample report so you can see the current status. We gather the information at domain based, and it shows total received emails, total rejected viruses, total rejected spam and percentages for both of the rejections. In the end of the report is a list of rejected viruses.

## 6. [2025] Traficom — AI Audit Aid (AAA)

- **年份 (Year)**: 2025
- **合作方 (Partner)**: Traficom
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Security
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: AI Audit Aid (AAA)

### 挑战描述 (Description)

Reinvent how you run audits cost-efficiently with a scalable and viable AI solution (e.g. AI agent /Generative AI/Visual audits)\n\nTraficom conducts annually a noticeable number of audits in aviation, maritime, road and rail traffic, and in the communications sector, including cyber security. Traditional auditing processes are slow and expensive — AI auditing can reduce costs by 50–70 % and accelerate processes from weeks to days. Human auditors can get tired, overlook details, or be subjective — AI remains consistent and detects even minor anomalies. We invite you to explore how AI could make auditing and certification faster, simpler, and more cost-efficient for public sector organizations.\nBonus: The winning team will have the opportunity to present their solution together with Traficom and Sitra at Slush, exploring potential continuation and real-world implementation.\n\nDesign and prototype an AI-assisted end-to-end auditing workflow for aviation organizations (focus: EASA Part-145 maintenance organizations). The system should: \ningest large manuals and evidence sets, \ncross-reference them against criteria from EU regulations (EASA Easy Access Rules), \nflag gaps/non-conformities, \nproduce a summary to support auditors’ decision making. \nInclude a comparison between regulatory versions and current manuals. \nProduce a comprehensive list of questions related to the regulation (in this case EASA Part-145 maintenance organizations) for manual check by auditor\nBONUS: create a “test bench” for industry to test their draft manuals’ compatibility against regulation before submitting their application to the authority.\n\nCase: Aviation (Jukka Hannola, Tapani Maukonen, Antti Uutinen)\nYour task is to design and prototype how AI could manage an end-to-end auditing process:\nProcess massive volumes of documents, reports, and regulations.\n\n\nCompare findings against criteria from EU regulations, AMC and GM (EASA Easy Access Rules).\n\n\nHighlight anomalies, risks, and improvement opportunities.\n\n\nSuggest how the same solution could be adapted across different sectors (maritime, rail, communications).

### 挑战洞察 (Challenge Insights)

The scope of requirements is large → developing/reinventing the process can save a noticeable amount of time and resources. Processes are fragmented across multiple regulations, standards, documents, and systems → can AI interpret them all efficiently? Companies expect faster, smoother certifying and auditing services → AI must improve efficiency while maintaining trust and reliability.

### 评审标准 (Judging Criteria)

Impact – efficiency gains, cost savings, and improved user experience.\nFeasibility – realistic concepts that could be implemented, acknowledging legal and structural limits.\nInnovation – bold ideas that rethink auditing from the beginning.\nTechnical execution & resilience (reproducibility, snapshot/cache, error handling), Transparency (explainable scoring, claim-evidence mapping), Evidence quality (correct standards references, page/section citations), Relevant alternatives (mini-compare and cross-sector scalability).

### 奖项设置 (Prizes)

We will reward the top three teams with €1500, €1000, and €500.

### 可用资源 (Resources)

Mentors: certifying & auditing specialists from Traficom Aviation - Antti Uutinen, Martti Salminen, Ilona Jylhä, Jukka Hannola (Tapani Maukonen)\nIndustry representative: (if available)\t\nAnd: Jarno Ilme, Jaana Mäkiniemi, Ville Räisänen.\nTraficom Aviation team will provide background information, process diagrams, practical insights, and highlight critical pitfalls.

### 关于合作方 (About Company)

Traficom is the Finnish Transport and Communications Agency. We ensure safe, sustainable, and efficient transport and communications systems. At Junction 2025, we want to challenge developers to reimagine how AI could transform auditing and bring government auditing services into a new era.

## 7. [2025] WithSecure — Reputation Recon: AI-Powered Software Trust Assessment

- **年份 (Year)**: 2025
- **合作方 (Partner)**: WithSecure
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Security
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Reputation Recon: AI-Powered Software Trust Assessment

### 挑战描述 (Description)

Build an AI assessor that turns a product name or URL into a CISO-ready trust brief with sources in minutes.\n\nSecurity teams and CISOs (Chief Information Security Officer) are constantly asked to approve new tools they’ve never seen before. They need accurate, concise, and source-grounded snapshots of a product’s security posture, fast. This challenge asks participants to build a GenAI-powered assessor that fetches reliable signals from the web and synthesizes them into a decision-ready brief. Help us move security from reactive firefighting to proactive enablement.\n\nNote: Teams may optionally evaluate emerging integration frameworks (e.g., MCP services) as targets of assessment, but MCP is not required.\n\nGiven minimal input (product name, vendor, or URL), build a system that:\n- Resolves the entity and vendor identity.\n- Classifies the software into a clear taxonomy (e.g., File sharing, GenAI tool, SaaS CRM, Endpoint agent).\n- Produces a concise security posture summary with citations.\n- Covers: description, usage, vendor reputation, CVE trend summaries (Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures), incidents/abuse signals, data handling/compliance, deployment/admin controls, and transparent 0–100 trust/risk score with rationale and confidence.\n- Suggests 1–2 safer alternatives with short rationale.\n\nDeliverables can be a CLI (Command Line Interface), service, or bonus web UI with compare-view. A lightweight local cache with timestamps and reproducibility is required.

### 挑战洞察 (Challenge Insights)

Focus on high-signal sources: vendor security/PSIRT pages (Product Security Incident Response Team), Terms of Service/Data Processing Agreement, (System and Organization Controls Type II), ISO attestations, reputable advisories/CERTs (Notices from Computer Emergency Response Teams or vendors), and CISA KEV (CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog). Guard against hallucinations by labeling vendor-stated vs. independent claims. When data is scarce, return “Insufficient public evidence.”

### 评审标准 (Judging Criteria)

Evaluation criteria: \n\nProblem-fit and readability; correct entity resolution and categorization; evidence quality; security posture synthesis (trends, incidents, compliance); technical execution and resilience; transparent scoring logic; relevant alternatives with mini-compare.\n\nFunctional Requirements\n- Minimal Input: Accept product name, vendor, or URL; resolve to canonical product.\n- Categorization: Assign at least one category from taxonomy with justification.\n- Security Posture: Summarize CVE trends (12–24mo), incidents/abuse, compliance posture, admin controls.\n- Trust/Risk Rating: Transparent 0–100 with rationale and confidence.\n- Alternatives: Suggest safer tools with short rationale.\n- Citations: ≤8 URLs, each mapped to claims.\n- Interface: CLI (Command Line Interface) or service; bonus simple compare UI.\n- Local Cache: Required with timestamps and reproducibility.\n\n\nNon-Functional Requirements\n- GenAI use: entity resolution, classification, summarization; guard against hallucinations.\n- Privacy: no customer PII; label vendor vs independent claims.\n- Reproducibility: deterministic prompts, snapshot mode, cached raw fetches.\n- Resilience: handle missing data, API rate limits, partial failures gracefully.\n\n\nEvaluation Rubric (100 pts)\n- Problem fit & clarity (15)\n- Entity resolution & categorization (20)\n- Evidence & citation quality (24)\n- Security posture synthesis (12)\n- Technical execution & resilience (15)\n- Trust/risk score transparency (8)\n- Alternatives & quick compare (6)\n\nWinning projects will emphasize clarity: a brief readable in under 2 minutes, ≤8 links, transparent scoring logic, and explicit evidence labeling.

### 奖项设置 (Prizes)

Prizes: €2,000 / €1,500 / €500 (Top-3 teams)

### 可用资源 (Resources)

Withsecure will provide:\n\nPre-prepared list of applications (names, optional binary hashes).\n\n\nGuidance on source prioritization and claim–evidence mapping.\n\n\nPublic references: CVE/CVSS (Common Vulnerability Scoring System) databases, CISA KEV, vendor security pages, disclosure/bug bounty sites.\n\n\nExample prompts for entity resolution and categorization.\n\n\nMentors on-site during the weekend (technical + domain expertise).\n\n\nBonus theme: assessing MCP-style integrations.\n\n\nRequired: lightweight local cache (JSON/SQLite), snapshot mode, deterministic parameters.

### 关于合作方 (About Company)

At WithSecure™, we believe cyber security should empower, not overwhelm. That’s why our Elements Cloud blends AI, expertise, and co-security to deliver results that matter. We’ve got your back — and we’ll make it easy.\n\nAt Junction 2025, we bring cutting-edge cyber expertise straight to the builders shaping tomorrow.
