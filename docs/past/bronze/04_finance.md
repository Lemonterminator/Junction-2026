## 2. [2017] Nordea — Banking reimagined - Dazzle the markets using our banking APIs

- **年份 (Year)**: 2017
- **合作方 (Partner)**: Nordea
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Finance
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Banking reimagined - Dazzle the markets using our banking APIs

### 挑战描述 (Description)

The banking industry is undergoing radical change. New technology, new market players, and new regulation are disrupting a traditional and uninspired industry. One of these changes, the revised Payment Service Directive (PSD2), forces banks to allow their customers to control how they want to access their accounts. We want to go beyond what the regulation states, as we see it having far-reaching consequences in ways that we could not even dream of. That’s where you come in. Use our banking APIs and other technologies out there to create an app that will dazzle the markets.

### 挑战洞察 (Challenge Insights)

Participating teams will receive access to the Nordea Open Banking developer portal, which contains the API documentation and sandbox. \n\nWe will also have our developers and banking industry experts present at the event for any advice and mentoring you may need.

### 评审标准 (Judging Criteria)

What you create is completely up to you, but we have outlined some areas that may offer you some inspiration:\n\n1. Turning old and traditional into interactive and intelligent\nWhat comes into your mind when you think of Life insurance? Boring, confusing, complicated? Maybe currently this is the case, but the future is wide open. This is where we feel you can actually make a difference by utilising your expertise in order to make life insurance more interactive and intelligent. For us, being more interactive and intelligent means increasing the customer value and finding new ways of communicating it.  \nThink what kind of data or technology you could use, and apply to make life insurance more interactive. How could your solution decrease the customer pains and enhance the gains? What is the one thing that you would like to remove/resolve/change if you were responsible for the insurance services of the future? We know that activity based life insurance (i.e.Vitality) is already yesterday's news - what could be the next big thing?\n \n2. Making saving and investments more personal\nAs the world becomes increasingly digitalised, the expectation of speed, relevance, simplicity, and proactive outreach increases. People buy their clothes, pay their bills, get entertained, guided and advised in digital channels, while the amount of traditional face-to-face interactions are decreasing. This is true also for our customers. However, in a world with an ever increasing amount of options, the need for personalised advice and the desire for relevant information grow, and at the same time it needs to be delivered in more inspiring ways than we are used to.\nCome up with an interactive and inspiring solution for how to create engagement, increase knowledge, interest and willingness to save and at the same time entertain!

## 5. [2018] EQ (Electric intelligence by Mercedes-Benz) — The Best Electric Vehicle Charging Experience

- **年份 (Year)**: 2018
- **合作方 (Partner)**: EQ (Electric intelligence by Mercedes-Benz)
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Finance
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: The Best Electric Vehicle Charging Experience

### 挑战描述 (Description)

Bob is an EV driver. He is travelling from Stuttgart to Amsterdam on a business trip. He starts his journey on Monday morning from Stuttgart. On the way, he charges at different charging stations @Public and @Highway. At the end of his journey, he charges @Hotel. The next day he charges @Work and starts his way back from Amsterdam to Stuttgart. At the end of his journey, he charges @Home.\nBob wants to experience the following:\nHe wants to reserve the charging stations on the way\nSince he is on a long haul, he wants to get updates about peak hours of charging stations usage, charging tariff, nearby POI in his app in advance so that he can plan his travel in a pleasant manner\nHe wants to have a seamless charging experience no matter where he charges his car @home, @hotel, @highway, @workplace\nHe wants to make online payments for charging via his mobile app.

### 挑战洞察 (Challenge Insights)

The transition to electrification is underway with over 2 million electric vehicles (EVs) on the road today. Charging a battery can take between 2 and 4 hours. With the increasing number of EVs on the road and with limited amount of charging stations it is a challenge how to improve the whole charging experience for our EV customers at the same time being competitive, innovative, creative and fast to market. Living up to the expectations of Mercedes-Benz being “The Best or Nothing”.                                                                                                                  Please note: Currently there are different charging service providers for each infrastructure à Public, Highway, Home, Work etc.\nHow can Bob have a seamless charging experience despite having different service providers and different IT applications in the background?\n\nPoints to consider:\nAC and DC charging\nDifferent charging service providers (CSP) with heterogeneous IT infrastructure\nIntegration of CSPs to Daimler Mobile and Vehicle platform

## 8. [2022] Nexi — Crack the Payments

- **年份 (Year)**: 2022
- **合作方 (Partner)**: Nexi
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Finance
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Crack the Payments

### 挑战描述 (Description)

Behind the scenes, there are different communication protocols, cryptographic standards, financial sector requirements, country regulations, etc. However, the end-user of the payment system sees and holds only the top of the iceberg: The Card.\\nCards operate by striping, tapping, and inserting. Are these methods of payment equally secure? Does it matter which type of card (Maestro, Visa, MasterCard) you use? Dig into the communication between the card and terminal and investigate how secure the payments truly are!\\nBuild the solution which reads the card data using NFC protocol. Ideally, it should show the parsed content and identify the security level of your card. The solution should be easy to use and explain what was found in detail!

- The most juicy information from the cards, - The understanding and explanation of the found data, - Ideas of how to improve the security of the payment transactions

## 9. [2025] City of Espoo — Business Advisory Service 2.0

- **年份 (Year)**: 2025
- **合作方 (Partner)**: City of Espoo
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Finance
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Business Advisory Service 2.0

### 挑战描述 (Description)

Create a business advisory service that helps anyone start a company in Espoo – smarter, smoother, and more inclusively.\n\nEspoo wants to be the best place for innovation, business and employment. Entrepreneurship is booming, and Espoo is already one of the best places in the Nordics to start and grow a business. Espoo is the home of half of university born startups in Finland. According to the city strategy, Espoo strives to support the growth of new business. One key service is to offer free, personal business advisory services to help people start and grow companies. We want to ensure they have the knowledge, skills, and networks to build thriving businesses, but today’s business advisory services needs help to scale and improve accessibility. How can we extend personal advisory services to serve a wider audience with human-centered design and AI to make the path of starting a business smarter, more inclusive, and fit for the digital age? We’re bringing this challenge to Junction 2025 to co-create bold new ways public services can truly empower the entrepreneurs of tomorrow.\nJoin us in reimagining how public services can empower tomorrow’s entrepreneurs! \n\nEspoo’s business advisory services are valuable and free for everyone, but there’s room to improve their overall experience, accessibility and impact of business advisory services. We see a few key challenges in the current service: \nKnowledge & network gaps: Many new entrepreneurs in Espoo, especially those from international backgrounds, may lack knowledge of Finnish business procedures, ways of getting funding for business idea, local networks and contacts when starting out. Also questions regarding residence permits, business culture, taxing, insurances and how to open a bank account are themes that many new entrepreneurs are facing, especially coming from abroad. \nPreparation & expectations: People often arrive at advisory meetings unsure of what’s expected of them, how to prepare for the meeting, what documents to bring, or how to articulate their needs. This uncertainty can limit the value of the session for both the entrepreneur and the advisor.  \nInclusivity issues: The service isn’t equally effective for everyone; for example, language barriers or different levels of business experience can make it harder for some clients to benefit fully or clients may be in need of multiple advisory sessions to get answers to all the questions they have.\nWe believe that thoughtful digital services before the live meeting can help address these issues by enabling better formulation of business ideas and understanding of local authority compliance, clearer communication, and more personalized guidance.

### 挑战洞察 (Challenge Insights)

How might we make it easier for aspiring entrepreneurs to find the right information, solve early-stage questions, and feel more confident in their business journey — even before the personal advisory meeting? \n\nWe’re especially interested in smart and intuitive solutions that clarify the steps to start a business and complement the city’s personal advisory services. At the same time, how might we enable advisors to offer more personalized, impactful support by having better insight into each entrepreneur’s needs and context? \n\nKey facts about Espoo linked to the challenges: \n\n- Espoo is the fastest-growing city in Finland with a population of 324 000. 25% of the population have an immigrant background\n\n- Espoo is the home for around 24 000 companies. Most are solo entrepreneurs or microbusinesses, around 1000 companies are SMEs and employ between 10-250 people, roughly 100 are big companies and corporates. \n\n- Business advisory services help around 2000 clients yearly to start or develop their business. Most clients are starting a new business, but also already existing businesses are advised on developing their business. \n\n- Nearly half of business advisory clients have an international background. \n\n- Yearly around 400-500 new businesses are founded with the help of Business Espoo business advisory services. \n\n- Startup soft landing services provided by Espoo, Helsinki and Vantaa help international startups locate in Finland. In the year 2025 19 international startups have relocated to Espoo through the service provided by the city.

### 评审标准 (Judging Criteria)

Judging criteria: To win this challenge, teams should aim to combine smart technology with great user experience and real-world impact. Our judges will evaluate solutions based on: \n\n- Clarity of concept – Is the idea well-defined and communicated clearly? A simple, clear solution often beats a complicated one. (Weight: 50 %)\n\n- Human-centered design – Is your solution user-friendly and inclusive for people of different backgrounds? (Weight: 30 %)\n\n- Impact & alignment with Espoo’s vision – Will the solution make a meaningful difference for entrepreneurs and advisors? Does it align with Espoo’s values of inclusivity and future-ready public services? (Weight: 20 %)\n\nWe value originality and practical impact. Both a creative approach and a plan for real implementation will score points. Show us that your idea could actually work in the context of Business Espoo’s services and help people — that’s how you win.

The winner also gets an exclusive opportunity to present their solution to the City of Espoo’s business development and innovation leaders. (In other words, your idea won’t just win money — it could get real visibility with the people who can help make it happen!) Runner-up teams will receive Espoo-themed swag bags and will be featured on the city’s communication channels (a nice boost for your project’s visibility and a presentation to the cities of Helsinki and Vantaa).
