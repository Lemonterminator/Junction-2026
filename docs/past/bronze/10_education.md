## 1. [2020] Stara & Helsingin kaupunki — Reboot Building Repairs

- **年份 (Year)**: 2020
- **合作方 (Partner)**: Stara & Helsingin kaupunki
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Education
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Reboot Building Repairs

### 挑战描述 (Description)

Each year, there are more than 4 000 smaller and bigger repair projects of the buildings owned by the City of Helsinki. The project is usually defined by real estate “owner” of City of Helsinki, planned by our engineer or supervisor and performed by a professional, such as an electrician, a painter, a plumber or a carpenter. We want to manage these projects in a better way, and step up the processes. The management process: 1. Real estate administrator of City of Helsinki orders the work , 2. initial planning of our own personnel, 3. preparing the entering to the site, 4. performing the actual work often in the middle of everyday life in the space and 5. documenting what has been done (pictures, bill of materials, planning documents, description of the result etc.).\n\nAlso informing the end users (such as teachers and a headmaster of a school, a parent of daycare child or an elderly people from their home) during the project is another key area of challenge.\n\nIt all comes down to two things: providing more information in advance, in order to minimize the number of planning visits to the site, and afterwards documenting the work, so that result can be controlled, even without another visit to the site.

### 可用资源 (Resources)

Stara will provide: We have mentors with practical hands-on experience and know-how of the data. We will provide detailed data from the processes. The data consists of actual examples, in a simple case pictures and some measurements, maybe a CAD drawing, and in more complex cases (read bigger ones) actual plans and drawings.

## 2. [2025] University of Helsinki: Helsinki Education Hub — Financial Literacy for All

- **年份 (Year)**: 2025
- **合作方 (Partner)**: University of Helsinki: Helsinki Education Hub
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Education
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Financial Literacy for All

### 挑战描述 (Description)

Reimagine how digital technologies and AI can empower young people with accessible, engaging, and effective financial literacy tools. \n\nIn Finland, the government has set a strategic goal: by 2030, Finland should be the most financially literate country in the world. While schools already touch on financial skills, teaching is fragmented, and disparities remain based on family background and gender. Youth need equal opportunities to build financial competence independent of their social and economic background. \n\nWith digital technologies and AI, we can create inclusive, engaging, and personalized tools that boost financial self-efficacy and decision-making skills. That’s why we’re bringing this challenge to Junction: to spark innovative solutions that help every youth succeed in steering their financial future. \n\n\nWe challenge you to design innovative digital tools that improve financial literacy for youth. The solution should be engaging and easy to test with students, educators, or young adults at key stages of independence (e.g., getting their first bank card, moving out from parents or getting their first summer job paycheck). The target group can include school students, vocational learners, or early young adults. Teachers and educators can also be part of the audience, but your solution should primarily focus on supporting youth. \n\nYour project can take any form e.g. an app, a chatbot, a gamified learning experience, or an AI-powered simulation – as long as it meaningfully supports financial skill-building and uses research-aligned learning methods.

### 挑战洞察 (Challenge Insights)

We’re especially interested in AI-driven personalization (tailoring guidance to individual needs), gamified simulations (learning-by-doing, e.g. “what if I use my first credit card?”), and confidence-building features (e.g. boosting financial self-efficacy for girls or increasing risk-awareness for boys).

### 评审标准 (Judging Criteria)

Winning solutions will be judged on: \nEducational effectiveness – Does it genuinely support learning in financial literacy? \nResearch-informed design – Is the idea backed by insights from studies or data? \nInnovation and feasibility – Is the solution unique, scalable, and realistic to continue after Junction? \nUser experience – Is it intuitive, engaging, and easy to test with the target group?

### 奖项设置 (Prizes)

The top three teams will be rewarded with cash prizes HEH 1500, 500, 500, alongside opportunities to join Helsinki Education Hub's unique, top-level incubator program where you can continue developing and scaling your solution with experienced researchers, business mentors, and industry experts.

### 可用资源 (Resources)

At the hackathon, you will have access to:\n\n\nSummaries, reports, and policy recommendations from financial literacy studies (in English).\n\n\nMentors from research, pedagogy, and the financial sector and to guide the content aspects.\n\n\nThematic guidance to help you frame your ideas in a research-informed way. \n\nWe are not providing specific datasets or devices; your creativity and multidisciplinary teamwork are the focus. However, existing research material and mentor support will help you validate and strengthen your ideas. \n\nExtra details:\nThis challenge builds on Helsinki Education Hub’s role in advancing innovative educational technologies. By connecting pedagogical research with developer creativity, we want to explore how AI-powered solutions can be rapidly prototyped and further developed in incubation programs.

### 关于合作方 (About Company)

Helsinki Education Hub (HEH) is a platform supporting Edtech innovation in Finland and beyond. We connect startups, researchers, educators, and students to co-create solutions that transform learning. By participating in Junction, we aim to combine pedagogical expertise with cutting-edge AI to improve financial literacy and empower youth with life skills that directly shape their independence and well-being.\nWe are particularly interested in AI in education, financial literacy, gamified learning, and multidisciplinary collaboration. Promising prototypes may continue development within our incubation and innovation programs.

## 3. [2025] Figma — Prompt to code anything you can imagine with Figma Make.

- **年份 (Year)**: 2025
- **合作方 (Partner)**: Figma
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Education
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: Prompt to code anything you can imagine with Figma Make.

### 挑战描述 (Description)

Start with an idea and prompt your way to a functional prototype or web app, fast.\n\nFigma Make empowers you to turn ideas into reality with AI-powered design tools—generate, iterate, and build faster, all in one creative space. How far can you push the creative boundaries, what insights and problems can you solve through quick iteration and collaboration, and how can you differentiate with design quality? The Figma Make challenge asks to push the boundaries and improve the speed and efficacy of your concept to design a development workflow. Share your creative insights and learnings through the process, we want to see what you make and how you got there.

### 挑战洞察 (Challenge Insights)

Use Figma Make to speed up your exploration of new ideas, prototyping, and differentiating your project. The winning project that utilizes Figma Make will be selected based on the craft and quality of their final project.

### 评审标准 (Judging Criteria)

The winner of the Figma Make Challenge will show how using Figma Make accelerated their workflow and elevated their project concept, design, and the craft of the front end experience. We will look closely at how Figma Make was used to explore and validate the concept, iterate and refine the experience, or create an elegant and polished design.\n\nOptional additions..\nCreative uses of Make\nElegant, high quality design and interactions \nCompelling insight on how Make was used to improve innovation and rapid iteration during the process\nExtra credit for the use of multiple Figma products (e.g. Figma AI, Figma MCP, design, etc.)

### 奖项设置 (Prizes)

The Figma Make Challenge reward winner will receive a €3000 cash prize.

### 可用资源 (Resources)

Figma will have mentors from the Figma Advocacy team and the Figma MCP product team at the event with deep experience in product design, prototyping, and product development.\n\nFigma will provide access to Figma Make for all hackathon participants through a free upgrade code. Each participant can upgrade a team within their Figma account to Figma Professional for the duration of the hackathon. This upgrade will give participants access to Figma’s entire suite of products and AI tools, including Figma Make.

### 关于合作方 (About Company)

Figma’s platform helps teams bring ideas to life—whether you're brainstorming, creating a prototype, translating designs into code, or iterating with AI. From idea to product, Figma empowers teams to streamline workflows, launch faster, and work together in real time from anywhere in the world.
