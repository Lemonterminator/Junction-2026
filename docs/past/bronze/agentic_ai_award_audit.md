This source log separates historical **challenge statements** from the **projects and awards** produced at an event. Track placement and the Junction grand prize are different awards. A challenge's advertised prize or judging criteria do not identify its winner. Entries below are limited to claims supported by publicly inspectable sources; missing awards remain unknown.

## Coverage gaps and interpretation

- No primary or participant-authored winner evidence was located in this audit for Agentic AI-relevant challenges from 2021 or 2023. Absence from this table is **unknown**, not evidence that no prize was awarded.
- Junction 2025 participant organization Data Guild reports challenge placements for Supercell, Traficom, SAS, Figma, and Valio; those announcements can be used as participant-reported leads, but individual award claims should be corroborated before adding detailed project descriptions.
- The available evidence does not support treating all 31 reclassified silver challenge prompts as past winning projects.
- Do not infer a technical stack, model, measured impact, user count, latency, architecture, team background, or award from the challenge statement. Keep those fields blank/unknown unless a project-specific source supports them.
