### 挑战描述 (Description)

There is a factory, far far away from Junction, producing valuable metals to meet our everyday needs. Winning teams will demonstrate how autonomous agents can truly think together—balancing energy efficiency, flow stability, and system safety in real time. Evaluation focuses on technical soundness, creativity of AI coordination, realistic integration with OPC UA and Digital Twin environments, and potential real-world impact on critical infrastructure. Bonus points for solutions that visualize decision-making clearly and show measurable energy savings under variable conditions.\n\nTechnical soundness and functionality (25%) – stability, efficiency, and correctness of the AI logic under realistic conditions.\n\n\nCreativity and AI coordination (25%) – originality in agent interaction, forecasting methods, and adaptive control strategies.\n\n\nReal-world applicability and integration (25%) – feasibility for deployment using OPC UA, Digital Twin, or similar industrial frameworks.\n\n\nClarity and impact demonstration (25%) – how clearly the solution communicates its logic, benefits, and measurable energy savings.

### 可用资源 (Resources)

Participants will have access to HSY’s historical wastewater and inflow datasets—real operational data spanning years of environmental variation. System communication and control will be implemented through the OPC UA protocol, the industrial standard for secure, real-time data exchange. Mentors from Valmet, HSY, and Aalto University will provide hands-on guidance in areas such as process automation, multi-agent architectures, and in future solutions can be tested with digital twin simulation. Teams are free to use any AI framework of their choice—from reinforcement learning environments to optimization libraries or custom-built agent systems.

### 关于合作方 (About Company)

Valmet Automation and HSY (Helsingin seudun ympäristöpalvelut) join forces to shape the future of smart infrastructure. Valmet Automation provides advanced distributed automation systems used in pulp, paper, and energy industries, while HSY operates Helsinki’s critical water and wastewater networks. Together, they aim to explore how AI and data-driven automation can optimize pumping, reduce energy consumption, and support sustainable urban operations. By taking part in Junction, they invite developers to co-create practical AI solutions that transform industrial and municipal systems into intelligent, self-learning networks.
