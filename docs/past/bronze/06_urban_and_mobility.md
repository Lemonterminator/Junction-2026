## 15. [2025] Denso — ChargeID: Trusted EV Services

- **年份 (Year)**: 2025
- **合作方 (Partner)**: Denso
- **原始赛道 (Historical Track)**: Urban & Mobility
- **挑战名称 (Challenge Name)**: ChargeID: Trusted EV Services

### 挑战描述 (Description)

Reimagine semi-public EV charging with AI and decentralized identity, where drivers, vehicles, and charge points interact seamlessly with businesses.\n\n\nEV charging in workplaces, fleets, and semi-public facilities is growing fast, yet most infrastructure is still “dumb.” Businesses can’t easily create differentiated services, while drivers expect transparency and control. With AI and decentralized identity across drivers, vehicles, and charge points, charging journey & experience can evolve into secure, personalized, and productive services that benefit everyone from drivers to charger point operators to business who own vehicles and chargers. \n\nToday, companies such as fleet operators, employers, or retail chains have limited ways to innovate around charging, since infrastructure is basic and data flows are restricted and controlled by individual parties. Drivers, vehicles, and charge points all need trusted ways to authenticate, share, and interact with services without relying on central intermediaries. How can AI and decentralized identity enable businesses to deliver services like demand forecasting, cost optimization, or personalized charging experiences, while keeping ownership close to the user and the assets involved?

### 挑战洞察 (Challenge Insights)

We are especially curious about solutions that transform charging activity into business value, from demand prediction to anomaly detection, combined with decentralized identity for drivers, vehicles, and charge points to enable secure, seamless interactions. Opportunities could include fleet optimization, tenant or employee charging-as-a-service, or gamified incentives that nudge drivers to charge at optimal times or locations.

### 评审标准 (Judging Criteria)

Winning teams will stand out through innovation and creativity in applying decentralized identity, AI, and connected vehicle data to real-world charging services. We’ll focus on usability, strong technical execution, and respect for privacy and data control, while also rewarding concepts with clear business impact and scalability potential.

### 奖项设置 (Prizes)

We will reward the top three teams with a total of €1000 in cash prizes, recognizing the most creative and impactful solutions. In addition to the prize, winners will gain visibility among industry experts and receive mentoring opportunities with our product and R&D teams working on decentralized identity in mobility services.

### 可用资源 (Resources)

Mentors from DID project R&D and innovation team.\n\nDuring the event, participants will develop innovative semi-public B2B2C EV charging solutions that combine AI and decentralized identity. The challenge centers around the Denso DID Agent, a leading identity-as-a-service platform enabling secure and privacy-preserving connectivity for vehicles and related assets. This technology allows trusted, anonymous interactions between drivers, vehicles, and charge points within Mobility-as-a-Service (MaaS) scenarios.\n\nParticipants will have access to the Denso DID Agent, its API documentation, and sample datasets representing vehicles and charging stations. These resources provide a foundation for exploring authentication, data exchange, and service creation using decentralized identity principles.\n\nExpert mentors from our product development and R&D teams, actively involved in DID initiatives across the automotive sector, will support teams throughout the event.

### 关于合作方 (About Company)

DENSO is a global Tier-1 automotive technology leader focused on advanced mobility, electrification, thermal systems, and intelligent AI-driven vehicle solutions. Our mission is to create safer, cleaner, and more efficient mobility ecosystems worldwide. We bring deep engineering heritage, extensive R&D capabilities, and access to real automotive data and hardware platforms. At Junction, we seek bold teams who can rethink mobility through software, AI, and sustainable engineering—and we are committed to co-developing the most promising solutions beyond the event.
