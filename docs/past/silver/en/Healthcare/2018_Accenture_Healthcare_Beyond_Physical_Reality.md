# [2018] Accenture — Healthcare Beyond Physical Reality

## Metadata

- **Year**: 2018
- **Challenge Partner**: Accenture
- **Original Historical Track**: Health
- **2026 Target Track**: Healthcare Track
- **Sub-theme**: Clinical Care, Digital Diagnostics & Chronic Disease Management
- **Core Theme**: In collaboration with Helsinki University Hospital (HUS), using extended reality (XR/VR/AR) for telemedicine training, pain distraction, and clinical guidance
- **Classification Rationale**: Originally in the Health track. Jointly released by Accenture and Helsinki University Hospital (HUS), exploring the use of XR technology to break geographic limits on medical resources, assist medical skills training, and alleviate patient pain and anxiety during treatment.

---

## Full Original Challenge Name

Healthcare Beyond Physical Reality

## Challenge Description

According to WHO, half of the world lacks access to essential health services due to long distances and high expenses. XR can help to improve access to medical training, distract patients from painful experiences and build empathy. It can also provide guidance and even break the laws of physics. Accenture and Helsinki University Hospital are looking for XR solutions that bring together patients, medical professionals and service providers without physical borders.
