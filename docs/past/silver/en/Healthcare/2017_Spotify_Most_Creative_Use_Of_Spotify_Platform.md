## Judging Criteria

Creativity is what we care about most.

Some example projects built on the Spotify Platform include:
Mangomoji (https://twitter.com/SpotifyPlatform/status/914523577612894208): How about using emojis to help you discover music?
SDOKW (https://developer.spotify.com/showcase/item/six-degrees-kanye-west/): How many degrees of separation between Kanye West and your favourite artist?
DDDJ (https://twitter.com/SpotifyPlatform/status/914530772412968960): How about generating playlists by reverse engineering into a physical DJ controller?
Musixmatch (https://developer.spotify.com/showcase/item/musixmatch/): How about showing you lyrics in real-time based on the Spotify client?

There’s a lot more in our developer showcase (https://developer.spotify.com/showcase/). We recommend checking out the Full Track Audio Analysis (https://developer.spotify.com/web-api/get-audio-analysis/) and Audio Features API (https://developer.spotify.com/web-api/get-audio-features/) endpoints, they’re the most exciting for sure!
