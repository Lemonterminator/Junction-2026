# [2019] CGI & Sitra — Reimagine Mental Health Care

## Metadata

- **Year**: 2019
- **Challenge Partner**: CGI & Sitra
- **Original Historical Track**: Health
- **2026 Target Track**: Healthcare Track
- **Sub-theme**: Mental Health, Workplace Wellbeing & Emotional Care
- **Core Theme**: Early intervention and cross-institutional collaborative services for adolescent mental health (depression, anxiety, stress) based on fair data economy principles
- **Classification Rationale**: Originally in the Health track. Focuses on the mental health crisis of depression, anxiety, and eating disorders faced by 20–25% of Finnish adolescents, building a user-consent-driven ecosystem for early prevention, psychological support, and multi-institutional collaboration.

## Full Original Challenge Name

Reimagine Mental Health Care

## Challenge Description

The challenge is to choose one or more of the problems and you can use whatever technologies you want to use. Try to find answers to: How and when we could strengthen young people's good mental health and reduce factors that undermine it? 
Who should provide the help and when?  
How we can provide the help youngsters need, keep them healthy and in the workforce? 
How could we provide help in different phases of youth? 
What kind of extra tools or services could we provide when young adults enter the workforce?
How could different institutions work together to provide seamless services through ecosystems to combat mental health issues? 

Remember that the solution should follow the fair data economy principles:
- data in a digital service or ecosystem are only shared through the individual’s or other data source’s consent.
- data sharing is made transparent by shared rules. Data is not shared with third parties.
- The individual has the possibility to track how their data is used and for what purpose.

## Challenge Insights

Mental disorder is the most significant health problem among the young people in Finland. According to THL, around 20-25 % of our young in Finland will have some kind of mental disorder before they become adults. The most typical mental disorders are anxiety, depression, eating disorder and stress. (Kunttu, Pesonen ja Saari 2017) The symptoms of depression is found among 15 % of young people, anxiety among 10%, 10-15% have repetitive suicidal thoughts and 3-5 % of them have tried to commit a suicide.(Tolmunen, 2019)

In Finland, the need for mental health care has increased rapidly during the past few years. Many studies propose that the young people do not have more problems than before, but their attitude is different compared to the earlier generations. They are not ashamed and do not have the attitude ‘’I need to survive on my own’’ and thus are more open for help. One of the problem is that we as a society do not start solving these problems early enough.

According to researcher Mattila-Holappa’s study, the number of early retirement of young people has been raising year by year for ten years now. Keeping young people in the workforce is also crucial for the economy: a young adult’s disability pension can have direct costs of around 0,5m€ along with other indirect costs.
