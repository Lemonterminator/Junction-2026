## Full Original Challenge Name

ThriveConnect: Redefine Work Well-being

## Challenge Description

Build the platform that matches talent with a workplace where they don’t just work—they thrive.

The mind breaks under the weight of work when it becomes too much. Yet, companies still don't pay enough attention to this. Now imagine a workplace where employee well-being is at the heart of everything. What would it look like? How would it work? What if we could see the truth behind the culture—the real investment in well-being—and instead of needing to recover from work, your work helped you recharge? We believe growth starts with healthy people. We’re inviting you to develop a platform that not only matches employees with companies based on skills but also prioritizes shared values and mental health practices.
