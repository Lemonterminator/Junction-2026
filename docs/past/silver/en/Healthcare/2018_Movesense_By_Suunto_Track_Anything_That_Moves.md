# [2018] Movesense By Suunto — Track Anything that Moves

## Metadata

- **Year**: 2018
- **Challenge Partner**: Movesense By Suunto
- **Original Historical Track**: Entertainment
- **2026 Target Track**: Healthcare Track
- **Sub-theme**: Active Living, Sports Health & Physical Rehabilitation
- **Core Theme**: Precise quantitative tracking of human motion biomechanics, athlete physiological metrics, and daily rehabilitation movements
- **Classification Rationale**: Although originally in the Entertainment track, this challenge uses Movesense sensors supporting ECG and nine-axis inertial measurement to track human motion posture and physiological load, with broad applications in sports medicine and rehabilitation physical therapy.

---

## Full Original Challenge Name

Track Anything that Moves

## Challenge Description

The world is full of things that move: an athlete’s body or sports equipment, a machine on a production line, a bridge under traffic, any of us in our daily life, or anything else that we can imagine. Often it would be highly valuable to know how and when these things move – or don’t move. What is the tracking solution that you think the world clearly needs but is still lacking? Build your demo and win great Suunto watches.
