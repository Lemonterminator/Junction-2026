## 1. Track Definition & Reorganization Criteria

# 2026 Healthcare Track — Historical Challenge Reorganization Archive (Silver Layer)

## Track Definition & Reorganization Criteria

**2026 Corresponding Track**: **Healthcare Track**

This directory presents a systematic reorganization and curation of all challenges related to **clinical care and assisted diagnosis, chronic disease and pain management, mental health and workplace well-being, smart eldercare and cognitive care, wearable biosensing, rehabilitation and healthy lifestyles (Healthcare, MedTech, BioSensing & Well-being)** from among the complete set of 147 historical challenges (Bronze Layer) across Hack Junction 2017–2025:

1. **Complete inclusion of all 14 challenges from the historical `Health` track**:
   - **Core biomedical, clinical care, and physical/mental well-being challenges (10)**: Planmeca digital dentistry and 3D virtual patients; Sitra health and IoT sensor data lake; Accenture x HUS (Helsinki University Hospital) extended reality (XR) telemedicine and pain distraction; CGI & Sitra adolescent mental health early intervention ecosystem; Reaktor youth mental health privacy-preserving tools; Huawei remote work physical and mental well-being (HMS Health Kit); TietoEvry non-pharmacological chronic pain management and opioid abuse prevention; Lääkärikeskus Aava workplace mental health and well-being matching platform; Pfizer x Aava migraine onset prediction based on biosignals (HRV/sleep) and digital behavior; and Kone gamified incentives for hospital/building access.
   - **Same-track archival entries and technical supplementary challenges from the historical `Health` track (4)**: To ensure 100% lossless coverage of the historical `03_health.md`, the following are fully retained as they were originally categorized under the Health track on the official website: Spotify (audio feature-based mood creativity), Cisco DNA Center (network application health visualization), Eiger Year 2222, and the Aiven cloud data infrastructure supplementary challenge — with their original positioning explicitly annotated in the classification notes.
2. **In-depth identification and aggregation of 14 healthcare/well-being/biosensing challenges from other historical tracks**:
   - **Clinical oncology and precision radiotherapy (AI & Data cross-track)**: Varian radiation therapy dose plan review and oncology physician workflow optimization.
   - **Smart eldercare, cognitive decline mitigation, and social isolation prevention (AI & Data cross-track)**: Aalto University voice AI companion for aging-in-place (AR4U project, supporting daily health inquiries and cognitive stimulation for older adults); CGI & City of Turku preventive healthcare and home care recommendations for frail elderly living alone.
   - **Mental health emotion recognition and human-factor health environments (AI & Data cross-track)**: Huawei Sense the Motion (sedentary behavior intervention for adolescents + wearable emotion recognition for mental health patients and clinical treatment plans); Helvar & VTT 24GHz millimeter-wave radar for contactless extraction of vital signs from office workers; Helvar human-centric lighting health regulation based on the Moodmetric electrodermal activity emotion ring.
   - **Biosensing, rehabilitation medicine, and thermotherapy/sleep recovery (IoT & Hardware / Entertainment cross-track)**: Suunto Movesense injury rehabilitation and health & well-being special category (Health & Wellbeing Category); Ericsson wearable vital sign monitoring and emergency distress signaling in disasters and traffic accidents; Pixieray infrared eye-tracking smart glasses for fatigue and stress detection; Harvia sauna thermotherapy (Healing with Heat) integrated with Oura Ring / Apple Health for sleep and physiological recovery improvement.
   - **Nutrition, diet, and hygiene/sanitation (Sustainability cross-track)**: K-Group and CGI & Kesko daily healthy diet guidance based on grocery retail and food ingredient data; Oras 20-second standard handwashing hygiene monitoring and personal health water usage tracking.

#### Description

The challenge is to choose one or more of the problems and you can use whatever technologies you want to use. Try to find answers to: How and when we could strengthen young people's good mental health and reduce factors that undermine it? 
Who should provide the help and when?  
How we can provide the help youngsters need, keep them healthy and in the workforce? 
How could we provide help in different phases of youth? 
What kind of extra tools or services could we provide when young adults enter the workforce?
How could different institutions work together to provide seamless services through ecosystems to combat mental health issues? 

Remember that the solution should follow the fair data economy principles:
- data in a digital service or ecosystem are only shared through the individual’s or other data source’s consent.
- data sharing is made transparent by shared rules. Data is not shared with third parties.
- The individual has the possibility to track how their data is used and for what purpose.

### 9. [2025] Pfizer x Lääkärikeskus Aava — Head Start

- **File**: [2025_Pfizer_Aava_Head_Start_Migraine_Prediction.md](./2025_Pfizer_Aava_Head_Start_Migraine_Prediction.md)
- **Original Track**: `Health` | **Sub-theme**: Clinical Care, Digital Diagnostics & Chronic Disease Management
- **Core Theme**: Pfizer and Aava joint challenge: predicting migraine onset using heart rate variability (HRV), sleep, and passive digital behavior
- **Classification Rationale**: Originally in the Health track. Jointly issued by multinational pharmaceutical giant Pfizer and Aava Medical Center, fusing wearable biometric signals (HRV, sleep) with passive digital behaviors such as screen time and calendar to transform migraine care from passive pain relief to precise pre-onset prediction.

#### Description

Use biometric signals and digital behavior to forecast migraine episodes before they hit.

Migraine affects over a billion people and are one of the leading causes of short-term disability worldwide. Despite this, many migraine patients fail to treat their migraine attacks with their current medication, and often wait until symptoms are unbearable. With your help, we aim to turn migraine care from reactive to predictive. This is your chance to help individuals stay productive, improve life quality for millions, and build solutions that could scale globally.

Design a prototype that predicts migraine onset using signals such as HRV, sleep patterns, screen time, digital calendar activity or any other passive, or contextual signal. The ideal solution should work in the background, minimize manual input, and empower users with insights into their own trigger patterns. You may also experiment with personal AI agents or layered UX, keeping the solution simple, sticky, and clinically meaningful.

#### Resources

Participants will get access to fact sheet about migraine and relevant research papers. Suggested data includes wearable metrics, digital calendar logs, screen time, and sleep. Support available from Pfizer's and Aava's team.

Pfizer and Aava have invested heavily in migraine R&D and digital health innovation. We’re excited to see how data and design can combine to create real-world impact. Our goal is to prototype digital health interventions that are predictive, practical, and personal. Whether it’s a mobile app, dashboard, or wearable integration – we’re looking for solutions that go beyond theory.
