## Metadata

- **Year**: 2022
- **Challenge Partner**: Huawei
- **Original Historical Track**: Health
- **2026 Target Track**: Healthcare Track
- **Sub-theme**: Mental Health, Workplace Wellbeing & Emotional Care

## Full Original Challenge Name

Work Remotely with Huawei

## Challenge Description

The foundation for Huawei success for the past 30 years is the focus on technological and engineering innovations driven by consumer needs, listed as the sixth most innovative company in the world by Boston Consulting Group (BCG). Moving forward Huawei will pursue the innovation driven by the consumer needs along with the shared vision for the future to achieve technological leadership along with the world.

## Challenge Insights

Mobile apps are essential technologies for digital innovation which can be achieved by putting knowledge to work in creating apps to solve real-life problems. During Junction 2022, they're challenging our participants to build a prototype mobile app/game that implements innovative ideas about remote working, using the recommended Huawei Mobile Service SDKs.\
\
The mobile app or game does not have to be complex in order to be effective. The app or game should try to solve real life problems about remote work. The app must include one of the recommended HMS SKD to qualify. See more about HMS Core: https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/develop/

## Judging Criteria

Rating rules: Total score (150 points) = Judging panel score (70 points) + HMS bonus points( 70) + Other bonus points (10 points),【Judging panel score】User experience (10 points): The submitted app is stable and functional, with impressive user interactions, a user-friendly interface, and appealing visuals and sounds. Originality (30 points): The submitted app is loaded with unique and innovative functions that cannot be found in similar apps, ensuring long-term user appeal. Social value (30 points): The submitted app addresses an issue of widespread concern, and offers new solutions for a specific user group and/or scenario.【HMS bonus points5 points will be added for integrating each of the following: Map Kit, Location Kit, 10 points will be added for integrating each of the following: Audio Editor Kit, Video Editor Kit, Health Kit, Drive Kit, ML Kit, AR Engine Kit,【Other bonus points) Other bonus points, which are capped at 10 points. 2 points will be added for integrating other HMS capabilities.
