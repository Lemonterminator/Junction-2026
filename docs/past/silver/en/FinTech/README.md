This directory presents a systematic reorganization and curation of all challenges related to **Open Banking & Digital Banking, Mobile Payments & Frictionless Settlement, Regulatory Technology (RegTech / SupTech), Web3 & Cryptocurrency Quantitative Trading, Data Asset Ownership & Monetization, Enterprise Financial Automation, Youth Financial Literacy Education, and Commercial Procurement & Transactions (Financial Technology, Payments, RegTech, Web3 & Digital Commerce)** from the complete set of 147 historical challenges (Bronze Layer) across Hack Junction 2017–2025:

1. **Full retention of all 9 challenges from the historical `Finance` track**: Encompassing SME financial and invoice automation (Procountor); EU PSD2 open banking APIs with intelligent insurance/wealth management (Nordea); digital commercial banking microservice architecture covering KYC/AML/anti-fraud/P2P lending (Holvi); social finance and gamified bill splitting (Bankify); cross-operator EV charging time-of-use pricing with mobile online payments (Mercedes-Benz EQ); Nordic mobile payment subscriptions and billing APIs (MobilePay); social commerce and embedded cross-border payments (VK x AliExpress); NFC bank card contactless payment protocol parsing and transaction security auditing (Nexi); and an AI advisor for entrepreneurs covering company registration, tax filing, bank account opening, and financing (City of Espoo).

2. **Deep mining and aggregation of 13 FinTech/payments/regulatory/crypto and commercial transaction challenges from other historical tracks**:
   - **Central-bank-level Regulatory Technology (RegTech / SupTech)**: The $99 trillion financial market regulatory challenge published by Suomen Pankki (Bank of Finland) and FIN-FSA (Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority) under the `AI & Data` track — leveraging AI to analyze 55GB of EU and national financial regulations, identify credit risk, liquidity risk, and operational risk in credit institutions, and eliminate regulatory conflicts.
   - **Urban frictionless payments and decentralized mobility billing settlement**: Frictionless urban POS payments for high-frequency consumption scenarios published by Poplatek under the `Urban & Mobility` track; DENSO's decentralized identity (DID)-based B2B2C Charging-as-a-Service with automated authentication, billing, and cost optimization.
   - **Web3, cryptocurrency quantitative market-making, and data asset monetization**: Cross-exchange (25+) cryptocurrency algorithmic market-making and quantitative trading signal assistant (Crypto Trading Helper) published by Gravity Team under the `Other` track; real-time on-chain data indexing (empowering NFT/DeFi high-frequency trading decisions) and Soulbound Tokens (SBT on-chain credit credentials and wallet privacy) published by BNB Chain under the `AI & Data` and `Security` tracks; data ownership monetization and `DATA` token revenue sharing via Web3 Data Unions by TX Streamr; personal data asset ownership and exchange for financial/commercial services (Mastering my Data) by Sitra.
   - **Financial literacy education for all (Consumer FinTech x EdTech)**: "Financial Literacy for All" published by the University of Helsinki under the `Education` track — responding to Finland's 2030 national strategy to rank first globally in financial literacy, utilizing AI to build simulations for youth's first bank/credit card usage, first salary savings planning, and investment risk awareness tools.
   - **Corporate venture matching, B2B industrial procurement, and e-commerce transaction optimization**: Intelligent business matching algorithms between Espoo startups and multinational enterprises; Cronvall's B2B industrial goods digital procurement platform with cost and carbon footprint transparency; Columbia Road's elimination of impulse purchases and erroneous buys (return-related financial losses) in e-commerce; Marimekko's official brand C2C pre-loved marketplace.

### 10. [2025] Suomen Pankki — Use AI to develop regulation

- **File**: [2025_Suomen_Pankki_Use_AI_To_Develop_Regulation.md](./2025_Suomen_Pankki_Use_AI_To_Develop_Regulation.md)
- **Original Track**: `AI & Data` | **Sub-theme**: RegTech, SupTech & Central Bank Compliance
- **Core Theme**: Bank of Finland and FIN-FSA: using AI to parse 55GB of EU and national financial regulations, and to map credit risk, liquidity risk, and operational risk regulatory requirements for credit institutions
- **Classification Rationale**: Although originally in the AI & Data track, this challenge is jointly issued by the Bank of Finland and the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority (FIN-FSA), targeting the $99 trillion financial market and providing 55GB of real EU and national financial regulatory data. It requires identifying operational risk, credit risk, and liquidity risk of credit financial institutions and eliminating regulatory overlaps and contradictions, making it the most core central bank-level RegTech/SupTech challenge.

#### Description

AI for the 99 trillion euro financial sector: Streamlining financial sector regulation and improving its efficiency

The financial markets landscape is evolving quickly. Do you want to shape its future?

Globally, there is a strong push to reassess and improve regulation. Use the power of AI and share your ideas on how to develop and streamline the regulation of financial corporations operating in the credit markets. With your expertise, you can help to create an entirely new approach to financial sector regulation – and reduce the cost of the regulatory burden without sacrificing high European standards and values.

The task for hackathon participants is: 
1) To develop a method for identifying and categorising the key risks (eg operational risk, credit risk, liquidity risk) of financial corporations granting credit 

2) To link these risks to the requirements set out in various regulatory documents 

3) To compare how the requirements concerning the same risk differ in different regulatory texts and whether there are overlaps or contradictions among them 

4) To analyse, which risks are subject to the most and the least requirements 

5) Finally, the teams must present an illustrative summary and visualisation of the risks, the related requirements, and possible areas for improvement.

#### Resources

During the event, participants will be creating GenAI powered data analysis solutions on top of GCP. 

As a participant, you’ll receive:

55GB of real world legislation and other relevant documents (eg EU-level regulations and directives, national legislation, requirements and guidelines by the competent authorities).
