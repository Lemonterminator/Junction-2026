## Full Original Challenge Name

Make Finance Fun. Once and for all. - We're looking for mini Banker apps/games that will make finance with your social network FUN

## Challenge Description

Looking for a Fun Challenge? As known, counting expenses and managing finance can be boring and troublesome. We're looking for mini Banker apps/games that will make finance with your friends MORE FUN. Banker app/game could be for example fun and fast way of: share expenses, smart spending (education perspective), peer to peer savings and loans..

## About the Partner

Bankify is founded one year-ago with a mission to make finance FUN, so that everyone wants to be a banker in their social group. The ‘Banker’ application links sociality and finance together conveniently. With the ‘Banker’ application our users are already earning money and having fun while sharing expenses with others. The application is free to use and the "Banker user" benefits from the service and can recommend it further to their own social groups.
