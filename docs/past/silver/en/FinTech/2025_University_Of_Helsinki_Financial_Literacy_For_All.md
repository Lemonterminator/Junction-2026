## Full Original Challenge Name

Financial Literacy for All

## Challenge Description

Reimagine how digital technologies and AI can empower young people with accessible, engaging, and effective financial literacy tools. 

In Finland, the government has set a strategic goal: by 2030, Finland should be the most financially literate country in the world. While schools already touch on financial skills, teaching is fragmented, and disparities remain based on family background and gender. Youth need equal opportunities to build financial competence independent of their social and economic background. 

With digital technologies and AI, we can create inclusive, engaging, and personalized tools that boost financial self-efficacy and decision-making skills. That’s why we’re bringing this challenge to Junction: to spark innovative solutions that help every youth succeed in steering their financial future. 

We challenge you to design innovative digital tools that improve financial literacy for youth. The solution should be engaging and easy to test with students, educators, or young adults at key stages of independence (e.g., getting their first bank card, moving out from parents or getting their first summer job paycheck). The target group can include school students, vocational learners, or early young adults. Teachers and educators can also be part of the audience, but your solution should primarily focus on supporting youth. 

Your project can take any form e.g. an app, a chatbot, a gamified learning experience, or an AI-powered simulation – as long as it meaningfully supports financial skill-building and uses research-aligned learning methods.

## Challenge Insights

We’re especially interested in AI-driven personalization (tailoring guidance to individual needs), gamified simulations (learning-by-doing, e.g. “what if I use my first credit card?”), and confidence-building features (e.g. boosting financial self-efficacy for girls or increasing risk-awareness for boys).

## Judging Criteria

Winning solutions will be judged on: 
Educational effectiveness – Does it genuinely support learning in financial literacy? 
Research-informed design – Is the idea backed by insights from studies or data? 
Innovation and feasibility – Is the solution unique, scalable, and realistic to continue after Junction? 
User experience – Is it intuitive, engaging, and easy to test with the target group?

## Prizes

The top three teams will be rewarded with cash prizes HEH 1500, 500, 500, alongside opportunities to join Helsinki Education Hub's unique, top-level incubator program where you can continue developing and scaling your solution with experienced researchers, business mentors, and industry experts.

## Resources

At the hackathon, you will have access to:

Summaries, reports, and policy recommendations from financial literacy studies (in English).

Mentors from research, pedagogy, and the financial sector and to guide the content aspects.

Thematic guidance to help you frame your ideas in a research-informed way. 

We are not providing specific datasets or devices; your creativity and multidisciplinary teamwork are the focus. However, existing research material and mentor support will help you validate and strengthen your ideas. 

Extra details:
This challenge builds on Helsinki Education Hub’s role in advancing innovative educational technologies. By connecting pedagogical research with developer creativity, we want to explore how AI-powered solutions can be rapidly prototyped and further developed in incubation programs.

## About the Partner

Helsinki Education Hub (HEH) is a platform supporting Edtech innovation in Finland and beyond. We connect startups, researchers, educators, and students to co-create solutions that transform learning. By participating in Junction, we aim to combine pedagogical expertise with cutting-edge AI to improve financial literacy and empower youth with life skills that directly shape their independence and well-being.
We are particularly interested in AI in education, financial literacy, gamified learning, and multidisciplinary collaboration. Promising prototypes may continue development within our incubation and innovation programs.
