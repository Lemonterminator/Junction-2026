## Full Original Challenge Name

The Best Electric Vehicle Charging Experience

## Challenge Description

Bob is an EV driver. He is travelling from Stuttgart to Amsterdam on a business trip. He starts his journey on Monday morning from Stuttgart. On the way, he charges at different charging stations @Public and @Highway. At the end of his journey, he charges @Hotel. The next day he charges @Work and starts his way back from Amsterdam to Stuttgart. At the end of his journey, he charges @Home.
Bob wants to experience the following:
He wants to reserve the charging stations on the way
Since he is on a long haul, he wants to get updates about peak hours of charging stations usage, charging tariff, nearby POI in his app in advance so that he can plan his travel in a pleasant manner
He wants to have a seamless charging experience no matter where he charges his car @home, @hotel, @highway, @workplace
He wants to make online payments for charging via his mobile app.

## Challenge Insights

The transition to electrification is underway with over 2 million electric vehicles (EVs) on the road today. Charging a battery can take between 2 and 4 hours. With the increasing number of EVs on the road and with limited amount of charging stations it is a challenge how to improve the whole charging experience for our EV customers at the same time being competitive, innovative, creative and fast to market. Living up to the expectations of Mercedes-Benz being “The Best or Nothing”.                                                                                                                  Please note: Currently there are different charging service providers for each infrastructure à Public, Highway, Home, Work etc.
How can Bob have a seamless charging experience despite having different service providers and different IT applications in the background?

Points to consider:
AC and DC charging
Different charging service providers (CSP) with heterogeneous IT infrastructure
Integration of CSPs to Daimler Mobile and Vehicle platform
