## Full Original Challenge Name

Crypto Trading Helper

## Challenge Description

Being one of the top crypto market makers and helping to improve the efficiency in the crypto space with various in-house solutions to keep track on the markets both in micro and macro levels, we see that there are many different solutions for different trading bots / helpers in different areas, but none of the ones existing have found an optimal solution to help companies like us to precisely measure the trends, volumes, different signals in the market or even twitter sentiment.

## Challenge Insights

We are a global crypto liquidity provider and algorithmic market maker. We trade digital assets listed on more than 25 Centralized Exchanges across the globe. Trading around 0.8% of worlds Crypto spot volume.

## Judging Criteria

The most important thing we are looking for is a technical solution/idea and concrete steps to make it go live. We are looking for a different perspectives of the market and different data sources that could be implemented to determine better pricing.
