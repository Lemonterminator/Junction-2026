## Metadata

- **Year**: 2019
- **Challenge Partner**: VK
- **Original Historical Track**: Finance
- **2026 Target Track**: FinTech Track
- **Sub-theme**: Social Commerce, B2B Procurement & Transaction Optimization
- **Core Theme**: Social commerce transactions combining VK Mini Apps, the largest European social network, with AliExpress payment and product APIs for global cross-border e-commerce

---

## Full Original Challenge Name

Discover Social Commerce with VK

## Challenge Description

The world’s largest online marketplace, established in Asia, meets Europe's largest social network: How social commerce on VK can be set up to create an excellent shopping experience for users.

VK provides a platform for social experiences including e.g. communication, content, thematic communities and useful services. AliExpress provides a wide range of millions of products for customers and businesses, but in addition to that, they provide cloud computing and payment services. Imagine all the solutions that combine the advantages of a social platform and the world’s largest online marketplace together. What is the social platform shopping experience that you have been only dreaming about?

## Resources

VK will provide: VK API (including social graph functions) and AliExpress Products API. Our mentors will assist by answering questions about API usage. We also suggest that participants use the VK Mini Apps platform, which is based on the VK UI components library, and allows cross-client services to be created easily.
