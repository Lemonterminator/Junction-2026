## Full Original Challenge Name

Banking reimagined - Dazzle the markets using our banking APIs

## Challenge Description

The banking industry is undergoing radical change. New technology, new market players, and new regulation are disrupting a traditional and uninspired industry. One of these changes, the revised Payment Service Directive (PSD2), forces banks to allow their customers to control how they want to access their accounts. We want to go beyond what the regulation states, as we see it having far-reaching consequences in ways that we could not even dream of. That’s where you come in. Use our banking APIs and other technologies out there to create an app that will dazzle the markets.

## Judging Criteria

What you create is completely up to you, but we have outlined some areas that may offer you some inspiration:

1. Turning old and traditional into interactive and intelligent
What comes into your mind when you think of Life insurance? Boring, confusing, complicated? Maybe currently this is the case, but the future is wide open. This is where we feel you can actually make a difference by utilising your expertise in order to make life insurance more interactive and intelligent. For us, being more interactive and intelligent means increasing the customer value and finding new ways of communicating it.  
Think what kind of data or technology you could use, and apply to make life insurance more interactive. How could your solution decrease the customer pains and enhance the gains? What is the one thing that you would like to remove/resolve/change if you were responsible for the insurance services of the future? We know that activity based life insurance (i.e.Vitality) is already yesterday's news - what could be the next big thing?
 
2. Making saving and investments more personal
As the world becomes increasingly digitalised, the expectation of speed, relevance, simplicity, and proactive outreach increases. People buy their clothes, pay their bills, get entertained, guided and advised in digital channels, while the amount of traditional face-to-face interactions are decreasing. This is true also for our customers. However, in a world with an ever increasing amount of options, the need for personalised advice and the desire for relevant information grow, and at the same time it needs to be delivered in more inspiring ways than we are used to.
Come up with an interactive and inspiring solution for how to create engagement, increase knowledge, interest and willingness to save and at the same time entertain!
