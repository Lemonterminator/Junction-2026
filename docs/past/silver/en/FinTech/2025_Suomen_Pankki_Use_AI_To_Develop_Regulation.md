## Full Original Challenge Name

Use AI to develop regulation

## Challenge Description

AI for the 99 trillion euro financial sector: Streamlining financial sector regulation and improving its efficiency

The financial markets landscape is evolving quickly. Do you want to shape its future?

Globally, there is a strong push to reassess and improve regulation. Use the power of AI and share your ideas on how to develop and streamline the regulation of financial corporations operating in the credit markets. With your expertise, you can help to create an entirely new approach to financial sector regulation – and reduce the cost of the regulatory burden without sacrificing high European standards and values.

The task for hackathon participants is: 
1) To develop a method for identifying and categorising the key risks (eg operational risk, credit risk, liquidity risk) of financial corporations granting credit 

2) To link these risks to the requirements set out in various regulatory documents 

3) To compare how the requirements concerning the same risk differ in different regulatory texts and whether there are overlaps or contradictions among them 

4) To analyse, which risks are subject to the most and the least requirements 

5) Finally, the teams must present an illustrative summary and visualisation of the risks, the related requirements, and possible areas for improvement.

## Challenge Insights

Possible overlaps and contradictions in risk definitions. Risk- / activity-based approach in regulation.

## Judging Criteria

Particular emphasis in the evaluation is placed on innovation, the repeatability of the solution, technical architecture and potential for further development. (40% of points) 

The assessment criteria also include the comprehensiveness of risk categories as well as the identification and analysis of overlaps and contradictions, especially regarding their impacts and suggestions for simplifications. We may use check questions to test the tool. (technical solution: 30% of points, testing with check questions: 10% of points) 

The project must clearly demonstrate the thought process from idea to implementation. Furthermore, the solution and its presentation must be easy to read and understand, and results must be based on the given data. (20% of points)

## About the Partner

The Bank of Finland is the national monetary authority and central bank of Finland, and part of the Eurosystem. The Bank of Finland advances financial stability. A stable price level, safe payment systems and a reliable financial system promote sustainable economic growth, employment and the well-being of Finns. In this age of disinformation, the Bank of Finland also plays a crucial role in producing and analysing high-quality data and research to enable better decisions for society.

The Bank of Finland invites you to explore how data, technology and innovation could contribute to the developing of financial sector regulation and policies. This is a highly topical issue, and we are curious to see how your ideas could promote efficient, clear and appropriately targeted regulation and policies.
