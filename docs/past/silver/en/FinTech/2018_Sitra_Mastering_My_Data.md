# [2018] Sitra — Mastering my Data

## Metadata

- **Year**: 2018
- **Challenge Partner**: Sitra
- **Original Historical Track**: Other
- **2026 Target Track**: FinTech Track
- **Sub-theme**: Web3, Crypto Trading, Tokenomics & Data Monetization
- **Core Theme**: Finnish national innovation fund Sitra's Fair Data Economy (IHAN): personal digital asset rights confirmation, authorized sharing, and exchange for personalized commercial/financial services
- **Classification Rationale**: Although originally in the Other track, it originates from Sitra's renowned Fair Data Economy program, exploring how individuals as owners of high-value data assets can authorize data, exchange value, and obtain tailored financial and commercial services.

---

## Full Original Challenge Name

Mastering my Data

## Challenge Description

Data has become the most valuable natural resource. Imagine a situation where all the data in the world would be available for innovative service designers and bright tech-gurus. Imagine a situation where you as a customer could have access to tailor-made, personalised service offerings which have harnessed all your digital data. This is what Sitra, the Finnish Innovation Fund, a think-and-do-tank, wants to try out in its challenge.
