# [2018] Poplatek — Frictionless Urban Payments

## Metadata

- **Year**: 2018
- **Challenge Partner**: Poplatek
- **Original Historical Track**: Urban & Mobility
- **2026 Target Track**: FinTech Track
- **Sub-theme**: Mobile Payments, Frictionless POS & Mobility Billing
- **Core Theme**: Frictionless urban payment terminals (POS) for high-frequency consumption scenarios (vending kiosks, shared mobility parking) initiated by Finnish payment technology provider Poplatek
- **Classification Rationale**: Although originally in the Urban & Mobility track, this challenge is initiated by Poplatek, a professional payment software and POS terminal technology company. Its core is to design 'Frictionless Urban Payments' and a next-generation POS settlement system that surpass traditional card swiping and mobile QR code scanning.

---

## Full Original Challenge Name

Frictionless Urban Payments

## Challenge Description

Create a point-of-sale with frictionless payments for the urban people rushing from one place to another!
The cities of the future will be larger, and city life will be even more hectic. The metropolitan areas are filled with services ranging from self-service pizza kiosks to car/bicycle/hoverboard parking. The current solutions, like card and mobile phone payments, require too much attention from the busy citizen. Unless everything will be paid using targeted outdoor advertising, we need to have zero-friction payments in the streets.
