# [2019] Columbia Road — Bring Mispurchases to and End

## Metadata

- **Year**: 2019
- **Challenge Partner**: Columbia Road
- **Original Historical Track**: Sustainability
- **2026 Target Track**: FinTech Track
- **Sub-theme**: Social Commerce, B2B Procurement & Transaction Optimization
- **Core Theme**: Digital retail and e-commerce transaction optimization: using AI to eliminate impulse purchases and mispurchases, reducing financial losses from returns
- **Classification Rationale**: Although originally in the Sustainability track, it is initiated by Columbia Road, a well-known European digital commerce and e-commerce growth consulting company, focusing on purchase decision optimization, conversion quality improvement, and return cost control in the e-commerce transaction chain.

## Full Original Challenge Name

Bring Mispurchases to and End

## Challenge Description

Digital retail suffers from a vast number of mispurchases; people buy products they don’t enjoy due to lack of possibilities to test, feel and evaluate products – and people purchase things in impulse, things they didn’t need in the first place.

All this creates a business model that is heavy on companies and a massive environmental problem, where products are shipped back and forth around the globe, and thrown out or destroyed without ever being used. How can we get rid of mispurchases?

We are living in a time when with the help of technology, data, AI and automation we can start solving these issues. How can technology help people to buy more consciously and companies to sell more sustainably? Possible questions to think about: How to try and evaluate products without shipping & return process? How to create (a digital) shopping experience that offers a better visual, feel and size reference of the products? How to optimise and personalise digital shopping to avoid unneeded purchases
