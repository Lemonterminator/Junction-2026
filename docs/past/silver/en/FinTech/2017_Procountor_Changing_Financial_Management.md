## Full Original Challenge Name

Changing the financial management landscape - Simplify financial management for entrepreneurs.

## Challenge Description

Let entrepreneurs focus on their business and competitive strengths by streamlining and automating financial management. Invoicing, payroll, cash flow and other financial management routines take time away from other aspects of the business. We look for ways to minimize the time spent on these routine tasks in favor of focusing on your domain.

## Challenge Insights

Procountor will provide access to APIs in a public test environment, as well as access to the core web application in that environment. We will also have mentors present to help you out!

## Judging Criteria

Example of possible hacks include a mobile application to create invoices using Procountor's APIs, or an OCR application that can scan invoices.
