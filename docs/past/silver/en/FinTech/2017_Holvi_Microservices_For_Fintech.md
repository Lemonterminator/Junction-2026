## Full Original Challenge Name

Microservices For Fintech - Develop a small service in the space of Financial Technology

## Challenge Description

A Financial Service has a lot of moving parts, and what the customer sees is just the tip of the Iceberg. 

While it may appear that a fintech is a single company, it is in fact a group of services that compound the end product. These components are very often startups themselves: Identity verification, Authentication, Operations, Marketing tools, Monitoring, money management, payment methods… and we’re just starting.

We want you to focus on any small service that is part of the FinTech ecosystem, and build something cool. With the technologies you want, with the API’s that you like the most.

## Challenge Insights

Holvi will provide mentorship and support in all corners of a fintech:

- Financial Systems (accounting, money management)
- Operations (Identification, monitoring)
- Customer Support
- Compliance, Risk, fraud and Anti Money Laundering management
- Product and Design

We will also provide a cluster for you to deploy and test your microservice. You just have to use Docker and any other technology you prefer.

## Judging Criteria

Match peer to peer lenders, alleviate the registration process, reduce fraud, helping a bank back office to be agile, provide advices to customers on your financial life... All these topics are open for hacking !

Also, with PSD2 Disruption, all banks will have an api by 2018. What will you do with it?
