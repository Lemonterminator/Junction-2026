## Full Original Challenge Name

Innovate Beyond Mobilepay

## Challenge Description

MobilePay offers solutions to everyday payment situations – with subscriptions and invoices being one of the latest products. Related MobilePay APIs have been in the market since 2017, allowing personal users to pay for their subscriptions and invoices via mobile phone on-the-go.
To innovate and expand to new contexts with our products, we are offering a challenge to improve or create a new use case for the APIs directed to the Nordic markets.
We want you to take flight without restraints: feel free to rethink even the fundamentals – starting from user journeys to merchant interactions! What would you do with 4,4 million registered users?
