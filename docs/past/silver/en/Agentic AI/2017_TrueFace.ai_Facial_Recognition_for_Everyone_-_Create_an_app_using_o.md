## Metadata

- **Year**: 2017
- **Challenge Partner**: TrueFace.ai
- **Original Historical Track**: AI & Data
- **2026 Target Track**: Agentic AI Track
- **Sub-theme**: V. Physical World Sensing, CV & Automation Agents
- **Core Theme**: Automated check-in, payment, and access control systems based on deep learning face recognition, emotion, and facial feature detection
- **Classification Rationale**: Leveraging TrueFace.ai's face comparison, group recognition, facial landmarks, and anti-spoofing detection capabilities to build smart interaction applications such as face payment, frictionless check-in, and emotion sensing.

## Full Original Challenge Name

Facial Recognition for Everyone - Create an app using one or multiple of TrueFace.ai facial recognition APIs

## Challenge Description

Facial recognition is taking over. Even Apple is part of it now! From paying with your face, authenticating with your face, detecting emotions, detecting face characteristics, surveillance, automatic check-in and much more, the use cases are endless.

Take part in this and apply your own application of our deep learning powered facial recognition.

## Challenge Insights

Participants will create inventive, valuable and impressive applications using one or a combination of:

Face Detect (detect 1 or more faces in an image)

Face Match (match 1 face against a database of faces - e.g. can be used as a replacement for login)

Face Identify (identify 1 or many faces - e.g. can be used to identify individuals in a crowd)

Raw Landmark detection (identify the facial characteristics, e.g. can be used to recommend a type of glasses based on face shape)

Spoof detection (detect when there is an attack, i.e. someone is trying to trick the technology by holding up a photo of someone else)

We will provide slack and email support during the event, for both the technical and non-technical side of things.

## Judging Criteria

Impress us with the creativity, originality, quality and use of your application using TrueFace.ai technology
