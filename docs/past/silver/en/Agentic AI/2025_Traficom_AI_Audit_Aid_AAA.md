## Full Original Challenge Name

AI Audit Aid (AAA)

## Challenge Description

Reinvent how you run audits cost-efficiently with a scalable and viable AI solution (e.g. AI agent /Generative AI/Visual audits)

Traficom conducts annually a noticeable number of audits in aviation, maritime, road and rail traffic, and in the communications sector, including cyber security. Traditional auditing processes are slow and expensive — AI auditing can reduce costs by 50–70 % and accelerate processes from weeks to days. Human auditors can get tired, overlook details, or be subjective — AI remains consistent and detects even minor anomalies. We invite you to explore how AI could make auditing and certification faster, simpler, and more cost-efficient for public sector organizations.
Bonus: The winning team will have the opportunity to present their solution together with Traficom and Sitra at Slush, exploring potential continuation and real-world implementation.

Design and prototype an AI-assisted end-to-end auditing workflow for aviation organizations (focus: EASA Part-145 maintenance organizations). The system should: 
ingest large manuals and evidence sets, 
cross-reference them against criteria from EU regulations (EASA Easy Access Rules), 
flag gaps/non-conformities, 
produce a summary to support auditors’ decision making. 
Include a comparison between regulatory versions and current manuals. 
Produce a comprehensive list of questions related to the regulation (in this case EASA Part-145 maintenance organizations) for manual check by auditor
BONUS: create a “test bench” for industry to test their draft manuals’ compatibility against regulation before submitting their application to the authority.

Case: Aviation (Jukka Hannola, Tapani Maukonen, Antti Uutinen)
Your task is to design and prototype how AI could manage an end-to-end auditing process:
Process massive volumes of documents, reports, and regulations.

Compare findings against criteria from EU regulations, AMC and GM (EASA Easy Access Rules).

Highlight anomalies, risks, and improvement opportunities.

Suggest how the same solution could be adapted across different sectors (maritime, rail, communications).

## Challenge Insights

The scope of requirements is large → developing/reinventing the process can save a noticeable amount of time and resources. Processes are fragmented across multiple regulations, standards, documents, and systems → can AI interpret them all efficiently? Companies expect faster, smoother certifying and auditing services → AI must improve efficiency while maintaining trust and reliability.

## Judging Criteria

Impact – efficiency gains, cost savings, and improved user experience.
Feasibility – realistic concepts that could be implemented, acknowledging legal and structural limits.
Innovation – bold ideas that rethink auditing from the beginning.
Technical execution & resilience (reproducibility, snapshot/cache, error handling), Transparency (explainable scoring, claim-evidence mapping), Evidence quality (correct standards references, page/section citations), Relevant alternatives (mini-compare and cross-sector scalability).
