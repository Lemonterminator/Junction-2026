## Full Original Challenge Name

Agentic AI with the flexibility of code and easyness of no-code

## Challenge Description

n8n is an open-source workflow automation platform that bridges the gap between no-code simplicity and full-code power. This challenge invites you to harness n8n to build agentic AI systems — intelligent workflows that can reason, decide, and act autonomously across APIs, apps, and data sources.

Your task is to design and implement an AI agent (or multi-agent system) that leverages n8n’s flexibility to perform meaningful real-world tasks — from automating research or customer interactions to coordinating multiple AI tools, APIs, or databases. Combine reasoning models with n8n’s modular workflows to demonstrate what’s possible when code and no-code meet agentic intelligence.

What we’re looking for

Innovative use of AI reasoning or planning inside n8n workflows

Creative integration of multiple APIs, LLMs, or data sources

Strong demonstration of autonomy or real-world usefulness

Bonus points for open-source reusability or showcasing how non-developers could extend your system

Getting Started
You can use n8n Cloud
 or self-host locally. Explore built-in AI nodes or integrate external APIs (OpenAI, Hugging Face, Anthropic, etc.) to create dynamic agentic flows.
