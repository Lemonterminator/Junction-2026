## Full Original Challenge Name

Aging with AI

## Challenge Description

Empower independence, connection, and dignity in later life. 

Reimagine how AI can support active lifestyles that maintain dignity, independence, and social engagement in later life.

By 2050, the world will have more people over 60 than children under 5, a historic demographic shift that will redefine our societies. Today, nearly one in three older adults experience loneliness or social isolation and this is also true across Finland. These are not just emotional burdens, loneliness is linked to poorer physical health, faster cognitive decline, and even higher mortality risk.
Artificial intelligence has the potential to transform aging, helping older adults stay connected, healthy, and independent. Beyond familiar AI voice assistants, we can imagine intelligent systems that truly understand, empower, and uplift people in later life

As populations age, societies everywhere are reimagining how we live, care, and connect. Aging well is not only about health, it is also about participation, autonomy, and belonging. How can technology help us create more inclusive, connected, and empowering futures for older adults?
 
Your challenge is to design AI-driven solutions that enable independent, meaningful living in later life. The solution can address anything from social engagement and mobility to safety, daily routines, or lifelong learning. Whether through smart environments, predictive analytics, social platforms, or creative digital tools, the aim is to make technology a genuine enabler of human connection and dignity.
 
There are no format limits, the most impactful ideas may combine digital, physical, and social innovation. What matters most is your creativity in using AI to make aging well a shared achievement across generations.

## Challenge Insights

We focus on a voice-based AI companion for use at home, so it’s ideal if solutions center around this technology. Other technologies can also be incorporated into the solution

Enabling social participation and two-way engagement with family, caregivers, or local communities

Encouraging physical activity, mobility and maintaining routines through personalized, voice-guided interactions

Providing companionship and mentally stimulating activities without creating a dependency on AI

Enabling discovery of local services, activities, or hobbies via natural voice interaction

## Judging Criteria

We’ll reward solutions that combine human insight and technological creativity.

Judges will look for:

- Impact: potential to improve daily life and well-being for older adults

- Feasibility: realism, scalability, and ethical awareness of the concept

- Implementation: how well the idea is translated into a working prototype or demonstrable concept

- Human-centered design: empathy, accessibility, and dignity in the user experience

- Innovation: originality and creative use of AI to support aging in place

The AR4U project at Aalto University explores how voice-based artificial intelligence can help older adults stay active, connected, and independent at home. The project develops and tests an AI companion and calling service that provides daily support, conversation, and well-being check-ins—making technology more personal, accessible, and engaging for everyone.
AR4U is an Research-to-Business (R2B) project at Aalto University, funded by Business Finland, aiming to transform academic research into commercial solutions.
