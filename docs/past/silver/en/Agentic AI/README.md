### Reorganization Criteria
This catalog comprehensively reviews all 147 historical challenge statements from Hack Junction 2017–2025, systematically identifying **31 agentic and generative automation challenges** across the `AI & Data` track as well as cross-track categories including `Urban & Mobility`, `Sustainability`, `Security`, `Education`, `Entertainment`, `Finance`, and `Other`. These are organized into five core directions:

1. **Autonomous Workflow Orchestration & Multi-Agent Collaboration Systems**: e.g., n8n's Agentic AI workflow combining code and no-code approaches; Valmet x HSY's multi-agent autonomous collaborative optimization for urban wastewater networks; Valio Aimo's predictive alerting + multilingual voice outbound negotiation agent; P&G's automated orchestration agent for multinational marketing campaigns; Featherless.ai's autonomous research and insight generation agent workflow powered by open-source LLMs; Ericsson's 5G fleet collaborative scheduling agent.

2. **Compliance Auditing, Security Reconnaissance & Enterprise Knowledge Agents**: e.g., Traficom's end-to-end AI auditing agent for aviation regulations (AAA); WithSecure's autonomous software trust reconnaissance and MCP evaluation agent for CISOs; Suomen Pankki's GenAI system for analyzing conflicts in financial regulatory statutes; Outokumpu's zero-hallucination sustainable enterprise insight assistant; Ericsson's automated threat intelligence hunter; Varian's intelligent review assistant for radiotherapy treatment plans.

3. **Multimodal Interaction, Voice Companionship & Spatial Perception Agents**: e.g., Snap Spectacles' real-world AR spatial perception companion; Aalto's voice companionship agent for home-based elderly care; ElevenLabs' lifelike voice interaction agent; SAP's enterprise-grade multimodal customer service agent; VK's future human-computer interaction assistant; City of Espoo's entrepreneurship advisor agent; University of Helsinki's AI financial literacy coach; Sitra's automated facilitation agent for digital democracy online discussions.

4. **Generative Creativity, Prompt-to-Code & AI-Native Game Agents**: e.g., Figma Make (and Figma MCP) Prompt-to-Code development agent; Supercell's AI-First native adaptive games with memory-enabled intelligent NPCs; Starstuff's real-time generative 3D playable world agent; Smartly.io's automated ad creative proliferation system; Wolt's local commerce AI experience engine.

5. **Physical World Perception, Machine Vision & Automated Control Agents**: e.g., KONE x Granlund's visual automated inventory agent for building MEP assets; Sensofusion's microsecond-level event camera real-time tracking agent; Helvar's fully automated ambient lighting and "Building Brain" autonomous control system; TrueFace.ai's intelligent visual identity verification; CGI x KONE x Meyer Turku's cruise service robot orchestration.

Innovative use of AI reasoning or planning inside n8n workflows

Creative integration of multiple APIs, LLMs, or data sources

Strong demonstration of autonomy or real-world usefulness

Bonus points for open-source reusability or showcasing how non-developers could extend your system

### 2. [2025] Valmet x HSY — Intelligent Flow Optimization

- **File**: [2025_Valmet_x_HSY_Intelligent_Flow_Optimization.md](./2025_Valmet_x_HSY_Intelligent_Flow_Optimization.md)
- **Original Track**: `Sustainability` | **Sub-theme**: I. Autonomous Workflow Orchestration & Multi-Agent Systems
- **Core Theme**: Real-time adaptive optimization control of urban wastewater pumping stations driven by Multi-Agent AI collaboration
- **Classification Rationale**: Originating from the Sustainability track, it explicitly requires building a Multi-Agent AI system (e.g., electricity price forecasting Agent, storage tank capacity management Agent, pump group flow allocation Agent collaborating and gaming) to autonomously control critical urban infrastructure via the OPC UA protocol.

#### Description

Build a multi-agent AI that autonomously minimizes wastewater pumping energy—one of the most critical processes in modern infrastructure—by forecasting inflow, power prices, and flow dynamics in real time.

Every city relies on invisible systems that keep life running—and wastewater management is one of the most critical. Pumps operate nonstop, consuming massive amounts of energy while facing the chaos of inflow, storms, and fluctuating electricity prices. What if these networks could think for themselves? With multi-agent AI, Valmet and HSY invite you to transform static infrastructure into a living system that predicts, adapts, and optimizes wastewater flow in real time.

Wastewater networks operate 24/7 and cannot stop — yet they hide untapped potential for intelligent optimization. Each pumping station must balance constant flow, limited storage, and fluctuating electricity prices while staying resilient to stormwater surges. Traditional automation follows fixed rules and ignores real-time changes and forecasts. Your task is to build a multi-agent AI system where autonomous agents plan pumping according to daily energy prices, coordinate flow between stations, and find the most energy-efficient pumping strategy without violating safety or process constraints. The goal: make one of society’s most critical infrastructure systems self-optimizing.

#### Resources

Participants will have access to HSY’s historical wastewater and inflow datasets—real operational data spanning years of environmental variation. System communication and control will be implemented through the OPC UA protocol, the industrial standard for secure, real-time data exchange. Mentors from Valmet, HSY, and Aalto University will provide hands-on guidance in areas such as process automation, multi-agent architectures, and in future solutions can be tested with digital twin simulation. Teams are free to use any AI framework of their choice—from reinforcement learning environments to optimization libraries or custom-built agent systems.

### 3. [2025] Valio Aimo — Zero-Fail Logistics

- **File**: [2025_Valio_Aimo_Zero-Fail_Logistics.md](./2025_Valio_Aimo_Zero-Fail_Logistics.md)
- **Original Track**: `AI & Data` | **Sub-theme**: I. Autonomous Workflow Orchestration & Multi-Agent Systems
- **Core Theme**: Out-of-stock risk prediction + multilingual voice-first autonomous outbound negotiation Agent + multimodal claims
- **Classification Rationale**: Requires building an end-to-end autonomous logistics customer service loop: when out-of-stock is detected during picking, the AI Agent automatically calls/emails the customer in their native language, proactively proposes substitute solutions and confirms modifications in real time, and can later automatically process claims via images/video.

#### Description

Reinvent delivery reliability with predictive AI and multilingual, automated customer care—voice first, text second.

Valio Aimo® serves 20,000+ professional kitchens. Today, missing items are often discovered at delivery or via next-morning manual calls—too late. At Junction 2025, we seek a multimodal AI solution (voice + text + image/video) that turns disruptions into proactive customer experiences and materially reduces operational workload.
Currently, when an ordered product is unavailable, customers only discover it at delivery or via a manual call the next day—too late. The goal is to automate the incomplete delivery process end-to-end.

Your challenge:

1. Pre-picking prediction – Use order, stock, and supplier/quality data to forecast which items are likely to be missing.

2. Proactive notice during picking – If a shortage is detected, an AI agent contacts the customer (call/text) in their language, proposes replacements, and confirms changes instantly.

3. Post-delivery remediation – If a shortage still occurs, the AI triggers after-the-fact outreach and arranges corrective delivery (same-day add-on run or next-day).

4. Multimodal claims – Explore voice, text, images, and video for handling delivery discrepancies.

Impact: This is not only about reducing manual work, but about saving an entire operations team’s workload while improving customer satisfaction.

#### Resources

Participants will have access to:

- Real-world order and picking data.

- “Mock data” extracts: order history, pick lines (timestamps), stock & availability, exception/quality logs, supplier delivery history.

- Test access to e-commerce / Minun Aimo for context (no separate sandbox).

- Multilingual APIs (ASR/TTS/NLU) and messaging channels (SMS/WhatsApp/email) for voice & text.

- Mentoring: AI, logistics, customer ops + partner coaching on which background data is essential.

Preferred tech: AI agents (voice & chat), predictive models, NLP/NLU, multimodal (image/video), optional routing/slot optimization.

#### Prizes

Prizes: Top three teams win €2000, €1500, and €500.
