## Full Original Challenge Name

Hack Human Interaction

## Challenge Description

Imagine how human to human (or human to machine) interaction will be in the future: AI-assisted, media-rich or clutter-free. How do you see it?
We challenge you to invent a way of human-to-human or human-to-machine interaction, a messenger of the future. Think AI-powered messaging, talking with bot assistants, sending only stories or audio, any type of communication other than a simple chat, and much more.
