## Challenge Description

Problem statement:
Wolt’s mission is to make cities better places to live. We connect people with the best local restaurants, stores, and couriers, and we’re constantly exploring how AI can make local commerce smarter, faster, and more personal.
Your challenge is to design and prototype an AI-powered solution that enhances how customers interact with their local city through Wolt.
The scope is open-ended: you can focus on the customer side of Wolt’s marketplace, but your solution should:
Be market-facing (i.e., something that could genuinely improve user experience or market efficiency).

Reflect real-world scalability (it should make sense in Wolt’s multi-country, multi-city ecosystem).

Showcase how AI or ML can unlock new value → through personalization, prediction, automation, or optimization.

Deliverables:

Teams of 4–5 should produce:
- A working prototype or simulation (no need to use real Wolt data → mock data or public sources are fine).

- A short presentation or demo explaining:

- The problem being solved.

- The AI/ML concept behind the solution.

- Why it matters for Wolt’s users and markets.

- How it could scale or integrate with Wolt’s platform.

## Judging Criteria

User & market impact - Does it make a real difference for the customer experience ?

Creativity & novelty - Is the idea original and reflective of Wolt’s way of solving problems?

Technical depth - Is the AI/ML implementation meaningful and well thought-out?

Scalability & feasibility - Could this concept work in multiple markets or product areas?
