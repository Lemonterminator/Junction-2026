## Challenge Description

Create a business advisory service that helps anyone start a company in Espoo – smarter, smoother, and more inclusively.

Espoo wants to be the best place for innovation, business and employment. Entrepreneurship is booming, and Espoo is already one of the best places in the Nordics to start and grow a business. Espoo is the home of half of university born startups in Finland. According to the city strategy, Espoo strives to support the growth of new business. One key service is to offer free, personal business advisory services to help people start and grow companies. We want to ensure they have the knowledge, skills, and networks to build thriving businesses, but today’s business advisory services needs help to scale and improve accessibility. How can we extend personal advisory services to serve a wider audience with human-centered design and AI to make the path of starting a business smarter, more inclusive, and fit for the digital age? We’re bringing this challenge to Junction 2025 to co-create bold new ways public services can truly empower the entrepreneurs of tomorrow.
Join us in reimagining how public services can empower tomorrow’s entrepreneurs! 

Espoo’s business advisory services are valuable and free for everyone, but there’s room to improve their overall experience, accessibility and impact of business advisory services. We see a few key challenges in the current service: 
Knowledge & network gaps: Many new entrepreneurs in Espoo, especially those from international backgrounds, may lack knowledge of Finnish business procedures, ways of getting funding for business idea, local networks and contacts when starting out. Also questions regarding residence permits, business culture, taxing, insurances and how to open a bank account are themes that many new entrepreneurs are facing, especially coming from abroad. 
Preparation & expectations: People often arrive at advisory meetings unsure of what’s expected of them, how to prepare for the meeting, what documents to bring, or how to articulate their needs. This uncertainty can limit the value of the session for both the entrepreneur and the advisor.  
Inclusivity issues: The service isn’t equally effective for everyone; for example, language barriers or different levels of business experience can make it harder for some clients to benefit fully or clients may be in need of multiple advisory sessions to get answers to all the questions they have.
We believe that thoughtful digital services before the live meeting can help address these issues by enabling better formulation of business ideas and understanding of local authority compliance, clearer communication, and more personalized guidance.

## Challenge Insights

How might we make it easier for aspiring entrepreneurs to find the right information, solve early-stage questions, and feel more confident in their business journey — even before the personal advisory meeting? 

We’re especially interested in smart and intuitive solutions that clarify the steps to start a business and complement the city’s personal advisory services. At the same time, how might we enable advisors to offer more personalized, impactful support by having better insight into each entrepreneur’s needs and context? 

Key facts about Espoo linked to the challenges: 

- Espoo is the fastest-growing city in Finland with a population of 324 000. 25% of the population have an immigrant background

- Espoo is the home for around 24 000 companies. Most are solo entrepreneurs or microbusinesses, around 1000 companies are SMEs and employ between 10-250 people, roughly 100 are big companies and corporates. 

- Business advisory services help around 2000 clients yearly to start or develop their business. Most clients are starting a new business, but also already existing businesses are advised on developing their business. 

- Nearly half of business advisory clients have an international background. 

- Yearly around 400-500 new businesses are founded with the help of Business Espoo business advisory services. 

- Startup soft landing services provided by Espoo, Helsinki and Vantaa help international startups locate in Finland. In the year 2025 19 international startups have relocated to Espoo through the service provided by the city.

## Judging Criteria

Judging criteria: To win this challenge, teams should aim to combine smart technology with great user experience and real-world impact. Our judges will evaluate solutions based on: 

- Clarity of concept – Is the idea well-defined and communicated clearly? A simple, clear solution often beats a complicated one. (Weight: 50 %)

- Human-centered design – Is your solution user-friendly and inclusive for people of different backgrounds? (Weight: 30 %)

- Impact & alignment with Espoo’s vision – Will the solution make a meaningful difference for entrepreneurs and advisors? Does it align with Espoo’s values of inclusivity and future-ready public services? (Weight: 20 %)

We value originality and practical impact. Both a creative approach and a plan for real implementation will score points. Show us that your idea could actually work in the context of Business Espoo’s services and help people — that’s how you win.

## Prizes

Prize: The winning team will receive €750 in cash , second runner up gets €500 and the third team €250. The winner also gets an exclusive opportunity to present their solution to the City of Espoo’s business development and innovation leaders. (In other words, your idea won’t just win money — it could get real visibility with the people who can help make it happen!) Runner-up teams will receive Espoo-themed swag bags and will be featured on the city’s communication channels (a nice boost for your project’s visibility and a presentation to the cities of Helsinki and Vantaa).

## Resources

Mentors: We will have mentors from the City of Espoo’s Business Advisory services team, from our Talent Team, who are helping international professionals find pathways and connect with the community in Espoo, as well as from Espoo’s digital innovation team. They will be on-site to coach teams in different aspects of your ideas. 

Participants will have access to a range of resources reflecting the real-world context of public business advisory services in Finland. You can explore existing service flows through the Business Espoo website and leverage open data from national sources. Here’s what we’ll provide to help you succeed: 

- Real-world data & content: Access to Business Espoo’s current service materials (e.g. advisory process descriptions, FAQs, business plan templates) and publicly available data sources related to starting a business – for example, information from the Finnish Enterprise Agencies (Uusyrityskeskus), the Finnish Patent and Registration Office (PRH), Business Finland, the Tax Administration (vero.fi), and Suomi.fi digital services. These resources will ground your solution in authentic content and requirements. 

- Tech tools & APIs: You can find a potentially useful Suomi.fi Service Information API in the list below. 

- Mentorship & expertise: Our mentors (business advisors, international talent experts and digital innovation project managers from the City of Espoo) will be available throughout the event to answer questions, provide feedback on your ideas, and ensure you have the domain information you need. They can help you understand the nuances of the problem space and guide you on feasible technical approaches.
 
- Testing opportunities: We’ll help teams validate their concepts against real-world scenarios. For instance, you can demonstrate your idea to an actual business advisor and test your solution’s effectiveness. This way, you can adjust your prototype to better fit user needs and city context. 

We’re excited to see creative, intuitive, and responsible uses of technology in your solutions – and we’re here to help you shape your ideas into something the City of Espoo could potentially pilot in the real world.

## About the Partner

The City of Espoo is one of Finland’s fastest growing and most innovative cities. We work actively to support entrepreneurship through the Business Espoo network, offering free advisory services, tools, and connections to help people start and grow their businesses. By participating in Junction 2025, we want to explore how technology and design can enhance the accessibility, quality and impact of these public services and ensure they are ready for the future. We’re excited to co-create with talented teams at Junction and are open to continuing collaboration beyond the hackathon. A great idea here could be the beginning of a real pilot in Espoo!
