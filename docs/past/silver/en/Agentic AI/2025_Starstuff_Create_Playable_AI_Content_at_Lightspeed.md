## Full Original Challenge Name

Create Playable AI Content at Lightspeed

With Starstuff, you can turn ideas into multiplayer, playable experiences in minutes - using AI, collaboration, and your imagination.

On any device, you can:

- Generate 3D models, textured assets, rigged humanoid characters, and full 360° worlds from prompts or photos

- Build simple multiplayer web games (shooter, racing, collecting, obby, etc.) using our custom functions and building blocks

- Test + play your game in real time with others directly in the browser

- Record, download and publish videos, and share your world instantly to others with a simple link

- Communicate easily with integrated voice and text chat

- Import .glb models (<10MB) or export your own characters and assets from your profile inventory

- We provide every Junction team with 1000 free Starbux AI creation credits (more available upon request).

Our Starstuff team will be onsite to help, debug, co-create, and hack our own beta with you - we’d love to learn from your ideas and feedback.

If you have any questions or want support during Junction, reach us at: help@starstuff.space
