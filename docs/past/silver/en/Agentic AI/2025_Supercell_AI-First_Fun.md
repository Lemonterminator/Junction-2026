## Challenge Description

Create an original game that uses AI to shape how it’s built, how it’s played, or how players experience it. 

At Supercell, we believe the best games are built by small, passionate teams who love what they create. For Junction 2025, we invite you to join our game jam and explore what happens when games and AI truly collide. This is your chance to imagine experiences that could only exist because of AI, games that think, react, and evolve in ways no designer could script alone. Whether you are a coder, artist, or storyteller, let AI become your creative partner and see how far you can push the boundaries of play.

Your mission is to create a game that could not exist without AI. How can intelligence, learning, or generative creativity become the core of the experience? Perhaps your world adapts to every player, your characters form memories and personalities, or your story unfolds differently each time. The goal is not perfection but exploration, to invent new kinds of play made possible only by AI. Surprise us with something fun, alive, unexpected, and uniquely yours.

## Resources

During the event, you will have access to mentors from Supercell’s game development, AI, and design teams, ready to share their experience and help you refine your ideas. You are free to use whatever tools or technologies you like, whether that means building with a commercial game engine like Unity or Unreal, creating something web-based, or experimenting with your own framework. It is all about creativity, exploration, and building something fun. Our mentors will be there to support and inspire you throughout the weekend.

## About the Partner

Supercell is a game company based in Helsinki, Finland, with offices in San Francisco, Seoul, Shanghai, and London. Since our launch in 2010, we've brought seven games to the global market – Hay Day, Clash of Clans, Boom Beach, Clash Royale, Brawl Stars, Squad Busters, and mo.co. Our dream is to create great games that as many people as possible play for years and that are remembered forever.
