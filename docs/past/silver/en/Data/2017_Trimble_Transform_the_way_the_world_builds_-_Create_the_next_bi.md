## Metadata

- **Year**: 2017
- **Challenge Partner**: Trimble
- **Original Historical Track**: AI & Data
- **2026 Target Track**: Data Track
- **Sub-theme**: IV. Building Digital Twins, Indoor mmWave Radar/Sensor Networks & 3D Engineering Data (Building Digital Twins, Radar & 3D Data)

## Full Original Challenge Name

Transform the way the world builds! - Create the next big thing in digitalizing the building industry.

## Challenge Description

We live in a 3D world, and we need to handle lots of 3D data in our business. The challenge is the journey from reality to digital and back to existing world. Have you ever measured your house or organized the furniture virtually using your phone? Now is the chance to put your fire into creating groundbreaking applications using 3D modelling, images, machine learning, sensors, augmented/virtual reality, voice recognition or whatever that makes things magical. Beam me up, Scotty!

## Challenge Insights

During the event Trimble technologies are available for the participants. For example, Tekla Structures, SketchUp, 3D Warehouse, Trimble Connect and other Trimble tools.

We will bring Microsoft HoloLenses and AR/VR gear into our track area.
