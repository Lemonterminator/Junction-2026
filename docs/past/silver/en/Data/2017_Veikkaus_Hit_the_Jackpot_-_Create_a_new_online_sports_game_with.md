## Full Original Challenge Name

Hit the Jackpot! - Create a new online sports game with our sports data.

## Challenge Description

What does a footballer think, when she’s running on the pitch, her eyes fixed on the ball? When the adrenaline pulses through ice-hockey players veins, and he knows that it’s up to his skills and tactics to score a goal? Or tennis-player serves an ace?

We challenge you to create a new sports-themed online game! Imagine how you would like to hit the jackpot? We will give you a massive amount of our sports data and statistics as well as help from our experts to add excitement to your game. Your game can be optimized for desktop, for mobile or for all devices. You decide what determines the player’s success in the game – is it based on chance, skills or both?

## Challenge Insights

We will bring a massive amount of our sports data and statistics as well as mentors to help with technical questions.

## Judging Criteria

Criteria for winning is to create an innovative sports game which has potential to become a new gaming product for Veikkaus. The game should hold intriguing logics but still be easy to understand. The visual experience would preferably inspire adults to play the game. We hope to see that the unique business model of Veikkaus is understood in your solutions.

## About the Partner

Veikkaus – we are a Finnish gaming company with a special mission. We offer entertaining and safe gaming experiences and take gaming-related detriments seriously. 

Veikkaus generates over one billion euros a year for the common good. We are the workplace to ca. 2,000 gaming industry professionals and welcome all developers and digital enthusiasts to further develop our gaming world and share their expertise in Junction.  

We have nearly 4,000 gaming locations and playing arcades, and we hold the exclusive right for gambling in Finland.
