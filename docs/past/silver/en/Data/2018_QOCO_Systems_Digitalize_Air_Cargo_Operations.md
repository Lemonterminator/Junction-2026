## Full Original Challenge Name

Digitalize Air Cargo Operations

## Challenge Description

The air cargo industry has many processes that could be optimized by digitalization. For example, the total carbon footprint of deliveries could be reduced by better use of available capacity.
At the moment, changes in tight schedules are solved manually. We believe that smart algorithms could help these people in their everyday jobs to make better, more efficient and faster decisions.
From the end users’ point of view, the current process of delivery tracking could be more transparent. It would be fair to notify customers about incoming delays as soon as possible.
