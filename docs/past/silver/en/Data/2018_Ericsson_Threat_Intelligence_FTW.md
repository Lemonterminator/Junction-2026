## Full Original Challenge Name

Threat Intelligence FTW

## Challenge Description

The amount of cyber-attacks is growing all the time. Security analysts spend a lot of their precious time looking for new threats, finding information about the threat actors and to connect the dots manually. Ericsson challenges you to jump into a security analyst’s stinky shoes (pun intended) and create a solution to deal with this almost unbearable task. Can you make a system that will smell the new threats before they reach you, and shed light to the structure and organization of the threat actors? Can you figure out what the internet has to say about this new threat actor you just stumbled upon? Can you combine indicators of compromise for these threats with the human readable information about the related threat actors all available on the internet?
There will be three focus areas in the challenge.
1. Choosing and information retrieval from different sources
2. Implementation of a data processing solution to filter and combine the relevant issues from retrieved sources
3. Presenting the resulting information to the security analyst in a valuable way
