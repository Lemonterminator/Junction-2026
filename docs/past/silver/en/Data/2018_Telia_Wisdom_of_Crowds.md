## Full Original Challenge Name

Wisdom of Crowds

## Challenge Description

Smartphones are an integral part of our lives. They keep us connected to our loved ones, help us to do our jobs and entertain us. They also generate lots of information about people movement behavior, which can be used to improve decision-making, build better services and optimize our society. We challenge you to innovate revolutionary services that combine our Crowd Insights data with other data sources and AI methods.
