## Full Original Challenge Name

Smooth Travels

## Challenge Description

As airlines are aiming to improve the customer experience through digital solutions, one crucial aspect is often forgotten: what happens to your luggage?

Being able to follow each step of your mail deliveries has long been a standard convenience, yet when it comes to luggage you’re left with just hoping it makes it to the end destination on time - and if it doesn’t, figuring out where it actually is can be quite tricky.

Millions of pieces of luggage get lost every year, and packing according to airline restrictions can be a pain. At the same time, airlines are tightening the restrictions on how much luggage you can bring in the cabin with you, encouraging flyers to check in their bags instead.

How could your team help improve the customer experience for flyers traveling with luggage?

We are looking for new ways to improve the existing baggage operations. Whether it's helping the passenger to pack lighter or track and reduce missing luggage, the solution should make traveling an easier, smoother and more comfortable experience.

## Challenge Insights

No idea where to start? Here are some ideas of what your solution could do:
Provide customers with more insight
Help airlines track the lost baggage (or even better; help prevent it from being lost in the first place!)
Help passengers to pack lighter and according to travel restrictions (packing restricted items is the main reason for having your luggage ransacked)
Help the baggage information share (tag > passenger > airline)
These are just some examples. Be creative, the sky's the limit!
