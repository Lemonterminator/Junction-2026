## Full Original Challenge Name

The Zero Waste Shop

## Challenge Description

Every day, sub-optimal planning leads to tons of food waste and excessive storage needs. This challenge is about using novel approaches like computer vision, or real-time point-of-sales data, to combat real life problems that retailers face. We want you to help us imagine completely new ways of collecting, analyzing and processing data, and create solutions that benefit both retailers and us. Step into our pop-up “Zero Waste Shop” and start collecting your data. You code, the planet benefits.
