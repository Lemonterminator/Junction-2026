## Metadata

- **Year**: 2018
- **Challenge Partner**: Aalto University // Acre
- **Original Historical Track**: AI & Data
- **2026 Target Track**: Data Track
- **Sub-theme**: III. Geospatial Analytics, Urban Big Data & People Flow
- **Core Theme**: People flow prediction, commercial leasing site selection, and precision marketing analysis based on campus-wide WiFi data at Otaniemi

## Full Original Challenge Name

Campus Services Under Hack

## Challenge Description

Understanding people flows is crucial for retailers to decide on a location as well as for real estate developers when planning services and retail. IoT solutions are making their way rapidly to the retail industry to revitalize physical stores while they have lost grounds to the e-commerce. Imagine if you could access the data of people’s behavior, habits, movements and daily routines within a whole city or a regional area – what possibilities would it bring to location-based services?
In this challenge you can access the wifi data of the whole Otaniemi campus area. Which data points could be combined and how could this knowledge affect the daily lives of students using the campus?
We are looking for new ways to help data-analytics in supporting leasing, retailer sales, on-spot advertisement, events and customer satisfaction on the Aalto University campus. The team with best knowledge and prediction capability on people flows wins the game.
Aalto University is building and developing a smart customer centric Aalto Campus, a campus area resembling a small city with technologies never seen or piloted before. One significant element in developing the area is the service infrastructure, a dynamic entity that provides quality services and creates revenue for the retailers. The new shopping center A Bloc is the central point of services in Otaniemi and aims to be a living lab in developing new technologies e.g. in predicting people flows and directing them in the wanted locations.
