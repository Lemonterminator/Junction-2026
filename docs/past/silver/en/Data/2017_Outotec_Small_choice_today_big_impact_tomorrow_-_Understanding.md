## Full Original Challenge Name

Small choice today, big impact tomorrow - Understanding the effect of our choices towards a sustainable metals industry

## Challenge Description

Mankind is exceeding Earth´s natural resource budget earlier and earlier every year. This means our current footprint on the planet is too heavy. We believe people will make wiser decisions, if they only had the relevant information available. At the same time massive amounts of open data sources are available from single satellites to social media and ethnographic information. How could we use this data to facilitate more sustainable choices?

Key focus area
Design a concept, tool or application to demonstrate the environmental footprint and social impact of producing, selecting and using materials.
