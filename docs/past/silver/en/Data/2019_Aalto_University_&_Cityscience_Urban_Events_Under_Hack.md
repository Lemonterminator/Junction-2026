## Full Original Challenge Name

Urban Events Under Hack

## Challenge Description

When organising events in large spaces there are myriads of interesting problems to solve. These include understanding social clustering such as managing access to the event, production, people flow, venue setup, communications etc. In Junction, CityScienceLab and the Aalto University campus challenge you to create the perfect event experience for participants in large scale venues.

Understanding people flows is crucial for location decisions of event organisers. Imagine if you could access the data of people’s behavior, habits, movements and daily routines within an event location – what possibilities would it bring to location-based services using wifi router data? Imagine which data points could be combined and how could knowledge of social patterns affect the daily lives of people on campus or at events? The one idea/demo with the best knowledge and/or prediction capability on people flows on campus wins the game.
