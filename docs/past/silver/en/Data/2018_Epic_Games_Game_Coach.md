## Challenge Description

Esports and gaming keep evolving, with players becoming ever more competitive in their games.
We want you to help gamers achieve their full potential, by leveraging player data from the free-to-play multiplayer online battle royale game, Darwin Project, developed and published by Scavengers Studios. Your challenge is to build a proof-of-concept for a digital coach for players looking to truly improve.
