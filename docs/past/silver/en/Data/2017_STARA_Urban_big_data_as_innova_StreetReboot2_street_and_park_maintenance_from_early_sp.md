## Metadata

- **Year**: 2017
- **Challenge Partner**: STARA: Urban big data as innovation platform in smart city context
- **Original Historical Track**: Urban & Mobility
- **2026 Target Track**: Data Track

## Full Original Challenge Name

StreetReboot2, street and park maintenance from early spring till late autumn - Developing warm season streets and parks production of services by big data in SmartCity context

## Challenge Description

There exist wide range of big data sources which has the capability to enhance cities street and park maintenance processes. "Urban big data as innovation platform in smart city context" strives to gasp such development.

## Judging Criteria

Innovative SmartCity data related mashups which are Six City Strategy scalable solutions related to fleet management such as vehicle maintenance forecast, SmartTrashbin routings, operational management & other data analytics.

## About the Partner

“Urban big data as innovation platform in smart city context” project is part of The Six City Strategy in which cities of Helsinki, Espoo, Tampere, Vantaa, Oulu and Turku aims to develop new type of services and innovations.
 “Urban big data as innovation platform in smart city context” is divided into three subprojects, which are carried out, by University of Tampere, City of Tampere and Helsinki City Construction Services Stara. 
Stara makes sure that we can all live in a clean and functional Helsinki. You can see us repairing buildings, planting trees and shrubberies, laying streets and cycling lanes and keeping the city clean. Whether you're moving about the city by car, bike or foot, Stara makes sure that the streets stay in good condition both in the summer and winter, come rain or shine.

STARA data theme in Hackjunction event relates into production of services for streets and parks during summer/warm season & fleet management & vehicle maintenance optimisation.
