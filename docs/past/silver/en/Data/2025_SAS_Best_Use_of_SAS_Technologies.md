Best Use of SAS Technologies

## Challenge Description

Create an impactful AI-driven solution using SAS Customer Intelligence 360 or SAS Viya.

SAS empowers organizations worldwide to turn data into insights and insights into action. In this challenge, we invite you to explore how SAS Customer Intelligence 360 and SAS Viya can be leveraged to build innovative, efficient, and intelligent solutions. Whether it’s optimizing decision-making, automating processes, or personalizing digital experiences, your creativity will define what’s possible with SAS technologies.

Your goal is to design and prototype a use case demonstrating the most creative and meaningful application of SAS Customer Intelligence 360 or SAS Viya. We’re looking for projects that highlight how these tools can harness data and AI to drive productivity, insights, and real-world impact. The problem domain is completely open—choose any industry, audience, or use case where smarter analytics can make a difference.

## Challenge Insights

We’re inspired by ideas that connect advanced analytics with tangible user or business value: personalized marketing journeys, data-driven decision optimization, and intelligent automation. Show how SAS tools can power tomorrow’s AI solutions.

## Judging Criteria

We will evaluate projects based on their creativity, real-world relevance, impact potential, and effective use of SAS platforms. The best project clearly demonstrates how SAS tools enhance productivity through AI and data-driven insight.
