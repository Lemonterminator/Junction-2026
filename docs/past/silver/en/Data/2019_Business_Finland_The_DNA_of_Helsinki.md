# [2019] Business Finland — The DNA of Helsinki

## Metadata

- **Year**: 2019
- **Challenge Partner**: Business Finland
- **Original Historical Track**: Urban & Mobility
- **2026 Target Track**: Data Track
- **Sub-theme**: III. Geospatial Analytics, Urban Big Data & People Flow
- **Core Theme**: Mining 1.7 billion rows of Helsinki urban dynamics data and port visitor trajectories, integrating POI, weather, and 3D city models to optimize transportation
- **Classification Rationale**: From the Urban & Mobility track, opening up to 1.7 billion rows of Helsinki city pulse data and port visitor mobility datasets, combined with open APIs for traffic flow, events, weather, etc., to reveal urban crowd movement patterns.

---

## Full Original Challenge Name

The DNA of Helsinki

## Challenge Description

Dive into 1,7 billion data rows of pulsing heart of Helsinki city. Identify behavioural patterns, from real-life city scale environment. Find ways to advance the perception of how the city dynamics work. Improve the city experience that will benefit the people as well as the planet.

Explore a large data set of tourist movement from Port of Helsinki. Discover key interest locations and behavioural models. Find ways to analyse the people flow between key locations and areas, and ways to optimize city services for example for transportation.

This challenge is looking for innovative ways to visualize any findings and novel ways to find useful  insights by combining data from different sources.

## Resources

Business finland will provide: REST API and API key  for accessing the data set, API Documentation, Technical support on-site, Links to supporting open data APIs from various sources, providing access to points of interest, events, traffic volumes, weather, 3D model of Helsinki and public transportation.
