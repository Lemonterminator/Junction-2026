## Metadata

- **Year**: 2019
- **Challenge Partner**: Microsoft
- **Original Historical Track**: AI & Data
- **2026 Target Track**: Data Track
- **Sub-theme**: III. Geospatial Analytics, Urban Big Data & People Flow
- **Core Theme**: Social impact analysis based on Finnish public transportation, air quality, urban 3D models, and social healthcare open datasets

## Full Original Challenge Name

Social Impact with Finland's Open Data

## Challenge Description

Finland has a variety of open data sets available, from public transportation, air quality, 3D-models of the cities, health, social services and more. We’re looking for projects that are either completely based on the open data sets (visualizing etc.), or leveraging open data as a part of a larger solution.

Solutions can be built with the technology of your choice, and leverage one or multiple Azure services in your solution, with a focus on data science techniques. Your solution can be built with the tools, languages, frameworks and platforms of your choice. You are free to apply to other challenges, and leverage Open Data in that solution to qualify for the Microsoft challenge.

We’re looking for a solution that leverages data science techniques on Azure, and our data & AI services.
