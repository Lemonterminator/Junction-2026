# [2018] City of Tampere — Towards the New Generation of Guidance

## Metadata

- **Year**: 2018
- **Challenge Partner**: City of Tampere
- **Original Historical Track**: Urban & Mobility
- **2026 Target Track**: Data Track
- **Sub-theme**: III. Geospatial Analytics, Urban Big Data & People Flow
- **Core Theme**: Urban service navigation, intelligent information screen diversion, and sustainable transportation guidance integrating multi-source people flow data
- **Classification Rationale**: From the Urban & Mobility track, integrating multi-source travel data to understand urban crowd movement patterns and translate them into dynamic guidance strategies on terminals such as urban information screens.

---

## Full Original Challenge Name

Towards the New Generation of Guidance

## Challenge Description

The City needs new guidance solutions to make guiding and finding services easier. Ideally, solutions will be based on specific data on how people move, hence the challenge includes how to get better understanding of how people are moving, by using innovative solutions and possibly combining different data sources. It is important to use gained information in developing guiding solutions such as the city info screens, in most challenging areas and situations.  At the same time, the target is to guide people towards sustainable modes of transport.
