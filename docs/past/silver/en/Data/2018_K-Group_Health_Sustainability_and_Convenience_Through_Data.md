# [2018] K-Group — Health, Sustainability and Convenience Through Data

## Metadata

- **Year**: 2018
- **Challenge Partner**: K-Group
- **Original Historical Track**: Sustainability
- **2026 Target Track**: Data Track
- **Sub-theme**: II. Retail Supply Chain, Logistics Scheduling & Sustainable Consumption Data Mining (Retail Supply Chain, Logistics & Consumption Data)
- **Core Theme**: Personalized health recommendations, origin traceability, and one-to-one marketing based on the massive retail transaction data pool of Finland's largest supermarket group
- **Classification Rationale**: From the Sustainability track, opens up K-Group's vast grocery and retail transaction data pool to mine personalized data services that support consumers' daily healthy eating and sustainable consumption.

## Full Original Challenge Name

Health, Sustainability and Convenience Through Data

## Challenge Description

As the world’s most sustainable trading sector company, we want to support our customers’ everyday life towards a more sustainable and healthier way. We have loads of retail transaction data from the grocery trade and other retail segments. There is a lot of potential in the customer-friendly utilisation of this data: we could create services that support our customers in making better choices daily.
We have an extensive data-pool and a desire to utilise it when developing new services, both data-based and others. Data enables us to offer customers a personalised, helpful and smooth customer experience in stores and online.
Customers expect 24/7 availability of services irrespective of place. Mobile access plays an important role. K Group aims to make use of these  possibilities of mobile and online services as well as digital marketing technologies and data that enable one-to-one marketing.
Consumer knowledge and power have increased, which requires clear customer promises and top-notch delivery from us. Thus, we want to provide comprehensive information about products, such as prices and availability, health benefits, and the origin and responsibility of our products. We take local tastes and needs into consideration when selecting the services and goods for a K-store and we particularly invest in the revamping of neighbourhood retail services.
We are committed to develop our business in a sustainable way by, for example, promoting the use of renewable energy and innovative circular economy solutions and ensuring responsibility throughout the supply chain.
