# [2019] Visit Finland — Finding ways to keep Finland's national parks enjoyable for both foreign travelers and locals

## Metadata

- **Year**: 2019
- **Challenge Partner**: Visit Finland
- **Original Historical Track**: Sustainability
- **2026 Target Track**: Data Track
- **Sub-theme**: III. Geospatial Analytics, Urban Big Data & People Flow
- **Core Theme**: Visitor flow prediction and diversion for scenic areas based on hourly visitor CSV data from trailheads of 40 Finnish national parks and social media popularity
- **Classification Rationale**: From the Sustainability track, providing hourly visitor volume CSV historical data for different route entrances of national parks, requiring combination with social media data to predict peak congestion and guide visitors to less crowded trails.

---

## Full Original Challenge Name

Finding ways to keep Finland's national parks enjoyable for both foreign travelers and locals

## Challenge Description

Nature travel and outdoors related activities are a trend in the travel industry. Visitor numbers to Finland’s 40 National Parks are growing and certain paths are getting very busy during peak times. Metsähallitus and Visit Finland joined forces and hope to find ways to make sure that travelers and locals can enjoy the unique and fragile national parks now and in the future. Smoothing out the people flow in crowded national parks might be one option, as well as increasing overall sustainability in the national parks.

How to make sure National Park visitors can enjoy the nature and their visit?
How to direct visitors also to the less used tracks and inform them about potential peaks and also low times? How to utilize social media to estimate visitor amounts?

## Resources

Visit Finland will provide: Hourly visitor data in CSV format, including e.g. the number of visitors in different National Parks route entrances.
