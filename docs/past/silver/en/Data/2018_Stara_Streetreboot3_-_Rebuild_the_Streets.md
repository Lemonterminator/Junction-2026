## Challenge Description

Blast street rebuilding projects! To do accomplish this, you’ll receive access to our massive smart city data. The main part of data is about a major, ongoing rebuilding project (that’s) located on Mechelininkatu, in central Helsinki. We encourage you to go wild with your ideas – that’s what we’re looking for.
