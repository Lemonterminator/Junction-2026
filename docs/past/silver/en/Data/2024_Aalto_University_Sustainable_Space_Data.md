## Full Original Challenge Name

Sustainable Space Data

## Challenge Description

Leverage open space data to create sustainable solutions for Earth's challenges.

Space agencies and organizations collect vast amounts of data from satellites, telescopes, and space missions, offering invaluable insights into Earth's climate, ecosystems, and even the broader solar system. However, much of this data remains untapped. By harnessing space data, we can discover innovative ways to solve global sustainability issues, from climate change to resource management. At Junction, we invite you to explore this wealth of information and create impactful solutions that benefit both Earth and space.
