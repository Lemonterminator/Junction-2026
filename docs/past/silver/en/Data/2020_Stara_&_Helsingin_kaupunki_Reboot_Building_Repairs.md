## Full Original Challenge Name

Reboot Building Repairs

## Challenge Description

Each year, there are more than 4 000 smaller and bigger repair projects of the buildings owned by the City of Helsinki. The project is usually defined by real estate “owner” of City of Helsinki, planned by our engineer or supervisor and performed by a professional, such as an electrician, a painter, a plumber or a carpenter. We want to manage these projects in a better way, and step up the processes. The management process: 1. Real estate administrator of City of Helsinki orders the work , 2. initial planning of our own personnel, 3. preparing the entering to the site, 4. performing the actual work often in the middle of everyday life in the space and 5. documenting what has been done (pictures, bill of materials, planning documents, description of the result etc.).

Also informing the end users (such as teachers and a headmaster of a school, a parent of daycare child or an elderly people from their home) during the project is another key area of challenge.

It all comes down to two things: providing more information in advance, in order to minimize the number of planning visits to the site, and afterwards documenting the work, so that result can be controlled, even without another visit to the site.

## Resources

Stara will provide: We have mentors with practical hands-on experience and know-how of the data. We will provide detailed data from the processes. The data consists of actual examples, in a simple case pictures and some measurements, maybe a CAD drawing, and in more complex cases (read bigger ones) actual plans and drawings.
