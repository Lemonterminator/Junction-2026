Revolutionize Building Design with IFC

Welcome to the Peikko challenge! Peikko, a global leader in concrete connections and composite structures, invites you to push the boundaries of innovation in the construction industry. We challenge you to harness the rich metadata of IFC files to transform how customers interact with building design and construction processes.
