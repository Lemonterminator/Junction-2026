# [2019] CGI & Kesko — Using Data to Help People Reduce Food Waste and Live Healthier Lives

## Metadata

- **Year**: 2019
- **Challenge Partner**: CGI & Kesko
- **Original Historical Track**: Sustainability
- **2026 Target Track**: Data Track
- **Sub-theme**: II. Retail Supply Chain, Logistics Scheduling & Sustainable Consumption Data Mining (Retail Supply Chain, Logistics & Consumption Data)
- **Core Theme**: Household waste reduction and healthy eating analysis based on anonymized supermarket shopping basket datasets and product recipe/price/availability APIs
- **Classification Rationale**: From the Sustainability track, provides Kesko's anonymized purchase basket dataset and retrieval, ingredient, price, and availability APIs, using transaction data analysis to help consumers reduce food waste.

---

## Full Original Challenge Name

Using Data to Help People Reduce Food Waste and Live Healthier Lives

## Challenge Description

This year at Junction, we are proud to announce that CGI is teaming up with K Group, the world’s most sustainable trading sector company. Together we want to find innovative ways to support consumers in making their everyday lives more sustainable and healthier.

Imagine the possibilities of using transaction data from grocery retail and other retail segments to create new services to support people in making healthier and more sustainable choices in their everyday lives. Can we find new ways to approach the global issue of reducing food waste and living a healthier lifestyle?

## Challenge Insights

People are striving to make more sustainable choices in their everyday lives and consumption. Healthy choices, as well as being able to reduce the amount of food waste, are becoming more and more important values in modern society.

As we know, climate change and increasing emissions raise concerns for many. However, lack of knowledge and tools restrict people from taking measures. It’s time for action and success requires cooperation.

## Resources

Kesko will provide: Several APIs for the challenge participants such as search, ingredients, pricing and availability. Other files and documentation such as fully anonymous purchase basket data will also be provided.
