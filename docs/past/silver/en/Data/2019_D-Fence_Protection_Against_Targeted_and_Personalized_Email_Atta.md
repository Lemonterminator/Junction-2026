## Full Original Challenge Name

Protection Against Targeted and Personalized Email Attacks

## Challenge Description

Cyber criminals and spammers pretend to be reliable senders, which creates an ongoing challenge for reliable identification of email messages. How it would be possible to identify them so that they could be classified?
