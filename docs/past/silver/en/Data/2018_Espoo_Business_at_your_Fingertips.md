## Full Original Challenge Name

Business at your Fingertips

## Challenge Description

The City of Espoo is looking for the best ways to help enterprises in matching and connecting to grow their business. Current challenges are:
  Startups development ideas don’t find suitable testing environments.
  Corporates don’t find new products, solutions and technologies
  Startups with novel solutions don’t find multinational corporates to gain corporate references.
  We need modern technology to boost the more systematic matching of startups and corporates.
What could be a solution for creating win-win situations for startups and corporates?
Particularly matching corporations with the right startups needs new innovations, e.g. an algorithm evaluating or predicting match qualities that could be used in our existing platform.
