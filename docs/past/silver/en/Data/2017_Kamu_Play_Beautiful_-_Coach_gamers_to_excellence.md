## Full Original Challenge Name

Play Beautiful - Coach gamers to excellence!

## Challenge Description

Competitive gaming is now larger than the NBA and NHL combined. Be part of this new branch of entertainment and help us leverage real-time data streams to provide gameplay analytics for both amateurs and professional gamers alike. Build upon these analytics to provide advanced tactical and strategical coaching advice or groundbreaking insight.

## Challenge Insights

You'll have access to real gameplay data from the gripping multiplayer PC game, team-based military experience, Squad. The data will be available as a real-time Kafka stream, as well as a static archive queryable from old-school MySQL. The data provides in-game coordinates, view angles, movement information, shooting events, damage indicators, and so on. In addition we provide Steam game keys and the entire set of game assets, such as the maps, weaponry, and any other the game specific information.

We are bringing mentors with years of experience in PC gaming and in developing AI/big data/web based solutions.

## Judging Criteria

With this challenge we wish to help gamers build their skills and reach their peak performance. Help us unlock insights in individual player game behaviors.
