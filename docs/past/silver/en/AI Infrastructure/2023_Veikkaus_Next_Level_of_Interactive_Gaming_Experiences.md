## Full Original Challenge Name

Next Level of Interactive Gaming Experiences

## Challenge Description

Veikkaus has been in the forefront of providing responsible online gambling through entertaining and safe gaming experiences. As a tech company, we are constantly challenged by providing new and innovative solutions, especially for online instant gaming. We are seeing interesting possibilities in replacing WebGL with WebGPU to provide developers the possibility to utilize more efficiently the GPU to achieve much more immersive and complex gaming experiences than what was possible with older technologies (WebGL). This is a real-life challenge we are working with and would love to hear how you would solve this! "The challenge is to create a technical POC utilizing WebGPU and WebWorker technologies. We do prefer that the nature of the POC looks like an online instant / scratch game, but any other implementation utilizing these technologies are very welcome. We’ll provide a curated list of learning resources and references to existing games you can benchmark on.  On top of this, we will have industry leading experts present to help you spar with both the concept of a next level online instant game and with possible technical questions."

## Challenge Insights

The basic principle for all online instant and scratch games are the same; player clicks or “scratch” to uncover symbols or combinations of them that match or reveal a winning result. The odds for the win are calculated in the backend, but the actual user interface for the gameplay should be as interesting and immersive as possible, keeping the player engaged and entertained to the end. The conventional way of providing the UI has been by using mainly WebGL, which starts to show its age as modern technologies are able to utilize the existing powerful system GPUs much more efficiently.

## Resources

The challenge is to create a technical POC utilizing WebGPU and WebWorker technologies. We do prefer that the nature of the POC looks like an online instant / scratch game, but any other implementation utilizing these technologies are very welcome. When thinking about your POC, you can draw inspiration from the demoscene (particle effects ftw) or from situations where complex, CPU-heavy tasks are required (like calculating winning chess moves). You’ll find some links to existing documentation about the technologies and links to existing games you can benchmark on. We will have industry leading experts present to help you spar with both the concept of a next level online instant game and with possible technical questions about creating the POC\
\
Regarding the technical implementation we hope that you will avoid external dependencies when possible. We would also hope that your entry would work directly in a browser and within an iFrame. \

Familiarity with other Graphics APIs, such as WebGL, Metal, Vulkan, or Direct3D, is not required, but if you have any experience with them, you'll likely notice lots of similarities with WebGPU that may help jump-start your learning!\
