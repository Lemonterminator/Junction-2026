## Full Original Challenge Name

Best use of Google Cloud

## Challenge Description

Use Google Cloud for your hackathon project. Take advantage of state-of-the-art machine learning tools, scalable infrastructure, a variety of serverless technologies, and easy-to-use devops tooling so that you can focus on your project challenge.There are often more than one way to solve a problem. Take your time and consider carefully“what is the problem you are solving” , “who will the solution be helping”. Once you have narrowed down answers to those questions, it’s easier to start selecting which technologies would be best for you.

## Judging Criteria

To get your hands on the prize, we expect you to demonstrate creative and innovative ways to combine Google Cloud services in your solution

## About the Partner

Google Cloud is a global leader in cloud computing, AI infrastructure, and data-driven innovation, powering organizations from startups to the world’s largest enterprises. The company focuses on enabling builders to deploy scalable architectures, leverage state-of-the-art AI models, and accelerate digital transformation securely. With deep resources—including advanced GPU/TPU infrastructure, industry-grade datasets, and world-class developer tooling—Google Cloud supports teams aiming to push the boundaries of applied AI. At Junction, Google Cloud participates to foster real experimentation on its platform and to identify next-generation solutions that can scale globally.
