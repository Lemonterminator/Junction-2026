## Full Original Challenge Name

[Side Challenge] Set up your project with Aiven!

## Challenge Description

At Junction 2022, we want to take care of the data infrastructure in your project. Aiven is included as a tool in Junction's Hackerpack, and the platform will be available for all participants to try out, free credits included.\
\
Our challenge is simple - launch your first Aiven service, get back to working on your project, and forget about managing your data infra!\
\
We'll reward the team who made the best use of Aiven's platform.

## Judging Criteria

The challenge winner will be the project that utilizes the Aiven platform in the most eloquent way (e.g. utilizing multiple services, having complexity in the set up or a creative use case). Please feel free to reach to out to the Aiven team on site or through the Junction Discord for tips and chats!

## Prizes

The best use of Aiven platform will be rewarded with a giftcard of 500 euros to Amazon!
