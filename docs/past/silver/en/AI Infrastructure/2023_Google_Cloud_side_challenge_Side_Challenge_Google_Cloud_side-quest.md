## Challenge Description

Use Google Cloud for your hackathon project. Take advantage of state-of-the-art machine learning tools, scalable infrastructure, a variety of serverless technologies, and easy-to-use devops tooling so that you can focus on your project challenge.There are often more than one way to solve a problem. Take your time and consider carefully“what is the problem you are solving” , “who will the solution be helping”. Once you have narrowed down answers to those questions, it’s easier to start selecting which technologies would be best for you.

## Judging Criteria

To get your hands on the prize, we expect you to demonstrate creative and innovative ways to combine Google Cloud services in your solution
