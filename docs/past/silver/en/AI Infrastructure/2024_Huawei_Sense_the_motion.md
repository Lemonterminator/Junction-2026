# [2024] Huawei — Sense the motion

## Metadata

- **Year**: 2024
- **Challenge Partner**: Huawei
- **Original Historical Track**: AI & Data
- **2026 Target Track**: AI Infrastructure Track (by Verda)
- **Sub-theme**: III. Edge Computing, 5G Networks & On-Device/WebGPU High-Performance Computing Foundation (Edge Computing, 5G & On-Device/WebGPU Runtime)
- **Core Theme**: Mobile/wearable device sensor frameworks and on-device real-time lightweight model deployment
- **Classification Rationale**: Emphasizes collecting real-time sensor streams on the STM SensorTile development board, Android Sensor Framework, and iOS Core Motion, and deploying emotion and motion recognition models to run in real time on wearable devices.

---

## Full Original Challenge Name

Sense the motion

## Challenge Description

Create innovative and captivating features for mobile and wearable devices that resonate with the youth, encouraging them to reduce sedentary behaviors and embrace more physical active lifestyle. In addition, for people suffering from mental health issues, emotion recognition could help improve self-awareness and mental health professionals could integrate the technology into treatment plans to monitor patient’s emotional response and well-being.

## Challenge Insights

Mobile phones have become inseparable companions in our daily lives. While they offer unparalleled convenience and entertainment, they often encourage sedentary behaviors. How can we turn our constant digital companions into catalysts for movement and healthier lifestyles?

## Judging Criteria

- Submit a project code and a demo video (or screen capture) showcasing the feature your team \
has developed. Please add audio or subtitle to the video that explains the feature verbally. Keep \
the length of the video to a maximum of 2 minutes.\
\
- 6-10 teams will be shortlisted and the selected teams will be invited to have a face-to-face \
meeting with the reviewers. During the meeting, each team will give a presentation demonstrating \
the purpose, process, and result of their work. Please keep the length of the presentation to a \
maximum of 5 minutes.\
\
- Solutions will be rated on their uniqueness, user impact, feasibility for deployment on \
mobile/wearables, completeness, engagement value, and fun factor. Each domain offers up to 10 \
points. Aim for creativity, tangible user benefits, practicality, compelling interaction, and a dash of \
excitement!\
\
- The main focus is on wearable devices but a solution can also be built on top of data collected from \
wearables and processed on mobile or even on backend. In addition, solution can be built based \
on open datasets and algorithm/model is then implemented to be used in real-time on wearable device.

## Prizes

1st place: 8000€ / 2nd place: 5000€ / 3rd place: 3000€

## Resources

During the event, participating teams will be creating novel sports and health features targeted to wearable or mobile devices. Feature, algorithm, or model can be created using some existing datasets or using real-time sensor data. Sensor data can be obtained from (but not limited to):\
\
- Android sensor framework\
- iOS Core Motion\
- STMicroelectronics sensor tile development kit\
- Free online datasets i.e. from Kaggle\
\
We are going to provide some STM sensor tile development kits to participants for development but otherwise, teams should use their own laptops, mobile phones, or smartwatches.\
