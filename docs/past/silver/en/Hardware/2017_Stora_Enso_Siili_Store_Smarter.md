- **Challenge Partner**: Stora Enso's challenge in cooperation with Siili Solutions

- **Sub-theme**: Spatial Positioning, RFID & Smart Terminal Hardware (RFID, Indoor Positioning & Smart Terminals)

## Full Original Challenge Name

Store smarter - Is there intelligent life in your space?

## Challenge Description

Do you have a storage space in your basement or in the attic? Do you remember what is in there? Nowadays we own so many things, and for many of us the experience of trying to organize our private or work stuff is challenging. The problem is that many times we don’t remember what we have, or we don’t know where those things are, or if it is still even usable. 

Sound familiar? Then why not help us come up with a smart storage space!
You can think of solving these problems, either for a
1)    Residential building (private storage space) or
2)    School, hospital, library or other public building (public storage spaces)

What would your smart storage space look like? Would it help reduce waste (like recycling or reuse)? Or keep track of and optimize the value of your items? Help in optimizing general warehouse management?

## Challenge Insights

RFID tags and reader(s), antennas and instructions how to use them and fetch the data from tags.
You will also have real storage space items to play around with. 

You can also discuss and get support from both Stora Enso team (business and tech) and Siili team (tech and design). We will also provide you with more info about Stora Enso products and technology that is used already. Siili mentors will help and spar your team in tech and design related matters.

## Judging Criteria

Some ideas to start your creative thinking

1)    Tracking/inventory system to find your stuff easily by using some device
2)    Get yourself (online) connected to your storage space and to your things
3)    Managing the space by using sensor technology and/or image/voice recognition…
4)    Creative solutions for reutilization/recycling/sharing of stored items

## About the Partner

Stora Enso is transforming from a traditional paper and board producer to a renewable materials growth company. In this exciting journey, new services and business models are being developed together with customers. For example, Intelligent Packaging by Stora Enso reinforces packaging with connectivity tags, readers, smart phone applications and scalable cloud-based software solutions. In Wood Products’ Building Solutions, high quality wooden building components, such as CLT or LVL are being equipped with sensors to make their tracking easier and to be able to monitor their humidity from production to building site and after installation. These are just a few examples of how digitalization is transforming our business throughout the supply chain.
