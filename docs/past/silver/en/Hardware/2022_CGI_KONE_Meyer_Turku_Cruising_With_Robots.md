# [2022] CGI & KONE & Meyer Turku — Cruising with Robots

## Metadata

- **Year**: 2022
- **Challenge Partner**: CGI & KONE & Meyer Turku
- **Original Historical Track**: Other
- **2026 Target Track**: Hardware Track (by Arduino)
- **Sub-theme**: Robotics, Heavy Machinery & Specialized Medical Hardware
- **Core Theme**: Design of autonomous service robots and automated passenger/cargo flow hardware systems on large luxury cruise ships
- **Classification Rationale**: Although originally in the Other track, it is jointly initiated by shipbuilding giant Meyer Turku, elevator and people-flow giant KONE, and CGI, with the core being the design of physical service robots and automated logistics hardware workflows in cruise ship scenarios.

---

## Full Original Challenge Name

Cruising with Robots

## Challenge Description

People flow is the way how people move in and between place A to place B. Good people flow means people moving smoothly, safely, comfortably, and without waiting. In cruises people flow can be discovered from the passengers’ and crew’s points of view.\
\
Think about the service that is visible and non-visible for cruise passengers. In conclusion, we wish to have a visual presentation (mock-ups, animation etc.) of the service path and people flow. You may use your imagination not only with robots but with automation systems, as well.\
\
We encourage you to be creative with your conclusion. Remember to put effort into the user interface in your solution via mock-ups.

## Judging Criteria

- creative, innovative and out-of-box ideas\
\
- service path description\
\
- how passenger experience has been considered\
\
- how much savings can be done with implementing this solution in a cruise ship (crew, space etc.)

## Winning Solutions & Verified Historical Cases

- **1st Place (Winner)**: **C-HAL 9000** `[L2]` *(named after HAL 9000 central AI orchestration; individual team roster and codebase not publicly archived)*
- **2nd Place**: **Laundry day is everyday** `[L2]` — Addressed non-visible high-workload cruise logistics (automated daily cabin linen & laundry circulation separated from passenger elevator peaks).
- **Verified Track Prototype Highlights (`[L2]` CGI Finland Blog)**: Spanning ~30 teams across CGI's 2022 tracks, hardware/system prototypes highlighted by CGI judges included **roof-climbing maintenance robots** and **passenger service-path tracking smartwatches** coupled with KONE people-flow & elevator orchestration.
- **Robotics & Integration Takeaways**: In multi-partner ship/building challenges (Meyer Turku × KONE × CGI), focusing on **non-visible high-frequency crew logistics** (like laundry turnover) and explicit **elevator/people-flow interfacing** beats generic lobby concierge delivery bots.
