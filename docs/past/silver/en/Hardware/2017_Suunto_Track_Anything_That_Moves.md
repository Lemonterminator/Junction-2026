## Full Original Challenge Name

Track anything that moves - Build a Movesense based tracking concept for sports, health or something else

## Challenge Description

The world is full of things that move – whether it’s an athlete’s body or sports equipment, a machine on a production line, a bridge under traffic, any of us in our daily life, or anything else that we can imagine. Often it would be highly valuable to know how and when these things move – or don’t move. Movesense offers a programmable motion sensor, API’s and software libraries that enable you to quickly build your own sensor functions and mobile applications to track anything that moves. Help the world to stay on the move!

## Challenge Insights

Participants will have an opportunity to build applications to the Movesense sensor and compatible mobile apps to iOS and Android. Sample apps for the sensor and mobile phone enable raw data collection and show how data can be processed and stored with the sensor and a mobile device. The whole system is based on simple REST API’s. The sensor is programmed with C++. A data connection with IBM Bluemix and Microsoft Azure will be available.
What we’ll bring:
-        A batch of Movesense sensors, enough for all projects. The sensor you use will be yours to keep after the hack if you submit a project to this challenge.
-        Programming jigs for fast upload and debugging of the sensor apps
-        Our Movesense software experts to support your work

## Judging Criteria

How can motion tracking help to optimize training of strength, speed, technique, balance, agility, tactics, or any other specific or generic area of sports performance that is not measured today?
What about health and wellbeing? There is much more than daily steps and calories that we need to know to live a healthy live or to recover after a health problem. Can you improve our life quality with motion tracking?
Or would you rather track something else than human beings? Optimize a production process? Digitalize agriculture? Improve logistics? There are so many moving things in the world. But, when and how to move or not to move? Well, that’s the question.

## About the Partner

Suunto is a Finnish manufacturer of world renowned sports watches, dive computers and precision instruments for the toughest conditions. Our new initiative, Movesense, is an open development environment and a programmable sensor for motion tracking and other sensoring for and beyond sports. With Movesense, you can commercialize your own sensor ideas quickly and cost effectively without having to worry about hardware development and manufacturing.
