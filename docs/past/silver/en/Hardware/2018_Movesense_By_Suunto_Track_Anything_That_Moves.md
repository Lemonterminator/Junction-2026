# [2018] Movesense By Suunto — Track Anything that Moves

## Metadata

- **Year**: 2018
- **Challenge Partner**: Movesense By Suunto
- **Original Historical Track**: Entertainment
- **2026 Target Track**: Hardware Track (by Arduino)
- **Sub-theme**: Wearables, Motion & Biometric Sensors
- **Core Theme**: Monitoring of sports equipment, human body, production lines, and bridges based on Movesense inertial measurement unit (IMU) sensors
- **Classification Rationale**: Although categorized in the Entertainment track in 2018, it shares the same origin as the 2017 Suunto challenge, with the core being the use of Movesense programmable motion sensor hardware to track athletes, sports equipment, industrial assembly line equipment, or bridge vibrations.

---

## Full Original Challenge Name

Track Anything that Moves

## Challenge Description

The world is full of things that move: an athlete’s body or sports equipment, a machine on a production line, a bridge under traffic, any of us in our daily life, or anything else that we can imagine. Often it would be highly valuable to know how and when these things move – or don’t move. What is the tracking solution that you think the world clearly needs but is still lacking? Build your demo and win great Suunto watches.

## Winning Solutions & Verified Historical Cases

*(26 submitted projects across 35 kits `[L1]` Movesense 2018 Recap)*:
- **1st Prize (Movesense Challenge)**: **Smart Labour Assistant** (Brian Irvine & Ahnaf Ahmad, Univ. of Oulu) `[L1]` — Paired chest respiratory-motion Movesense telemetry with **Unity 3D AR** biofeedback (a virtual bird tracking breathing cycles) to assist mothers during labor.
- **1st Prize (Movesense Multi-Challenge Combination) & 2nd Place (€1,000) Stara `StreetReboot2`**: **TerrainSense** (Ville Saramaa et al.) `[L1]`/`[L2]` — Mounted a Movesense IMU directly onto a bicycle's rear axle (`taka-akseli`) paired with a handlebar Android GPS node to map municipal bike-path roughness (`GPX` + web heatmaps on AWS); later scaled into pilots (through 2023) with Helsinki, Forum Virium Helsinki, HAMK, and InnoTrafik adding AWS Rekognition CV pothole analysis.
- **3rd Highlight (Movesense)**: **Take the Guessing Out of Coaching** `[L1]` — Collected **1,000** labeled basketball passes on-site from a wrist Movesense IMU and trained a neural network classifying passes vs. catches at **96.5% accuracy**.
- **Track Overall Champions Built on Movesense (`[L1]`)**: **Team GaitSense** (**HealthTech Track Overall Winner** — AI gait classifier) & **Team Health.io / Help.io** (**IoT Track Overall Winner**, **Intel 1st Prize**, **2nd Place Overall** — Movesense BLE triage + Intel Bluetooth Mesh).
