## 2. Core Products & Technical Architecture

Aalto University breaks down traditional academic silos, fusing **technology, business, and art & design** into a deeply integrated campus ecosystem.

### 2.1 The Six Schools Architecture
1. **School of Arts, Design and Architecture (ARTS)**: Spearheading global design thinking, spatial architecture, cinematic media, and radical creative expression.
2. **School of Business (BIZ)**: The only Nordic business school maintaining prestigious "Triple Crown" international accreditation (AACSB, AMBA, EQUIS).
3. **School of Chemical Engineering (CHEM)**: Deep focus on green chemistry, circular bio-based materials (such as Ioncell textile fibers), and clean energy conversion.
4. **School of Electrical Engineering (ELEC)**: Driving next-generation 5G/6G wireless networks, nanosatellites, autonomous microelectronics, and smart energy grids.
5. **School of Engineering (ENG)**: Encompassing mechanical engineering, Arctic marine and naval architecture, sustainable civil infrastructure, and smart mobility.
6. **School of Science (SCI)**: Frontier research base for computer science, artificial intelligence, applied physics (quantum computing and superconductivity), neuroscience, and human-computer interaction (HCI).

### 2.2 National Core Frontier Research Clusters
1. **Finnish Center for Artificial Intelligence (FCAI)**:
   - A national flagship initiative led by Aalto University in partnership with the University of Helsinki and VTT Technical Research Centre of Finland. Focuses on probabilistic machine learning, sample-efficient learning, human-AI collaborative decision-making, and trustworthy, explainable AI.
2. **Quantum Technology Ecosystem (InstituteQ & Q-Acc)**:
   - Co-operating Finland's first superconducting quantum computer, **Helmi**, in partnership with CSC – IT Center for Science and VTT, establishing a pan-European quantum computing and algorithm sandbox on the Otaniemi campus.
3. **Research-to-Business (R2B) Commercialization Platform**:
   - Backed substantially by Business Finland, dedicated to translating laboratory breakthroughs, software algorithms, and hardware prototypes into high-growth, venture-backed deep-tech enterprises (such as the eldercare conversational AI companion initiative **AR4U**).

In the Junction hackathon ecosystem, Aalto University serves simultaneously as the **Founding Cradle & Academic Heart** and as one of the most intellectually respected **Research-to-Business (R2B) Challenge Partners**.

### 5.3 Challenge Proposals

#### Proposal 1: Smart Aging & Multi-Sensory Cognitive Health Companion (AR4U Next: Eldercare & Multimodal Cognitive Health Guardian)
- **Problem Statement**: As demographic aging accelerates across Europe and globally, older adults confront severe loneliness, delayed detection of cognitive decline, and vulnerabilities to domestic fall incidents. Aalto's R2B project AR4U investigates ambient conversational and affective AI systems tailored for senior domestic environments.
- **Task Objective**: Leveraging AR4U's human-computer interaction research on aging, participating teams must architect an **intelligent voice companion application that bridges cognitive stimulation with proactive health monitoring**. Through empathetic daily conversation, interactive storytelling, and memory exercises, the system utilizes long-context, multimodal affective foundation models to detect micro-deviations in speech pacing, semantic fluency, or mood patterns. This enables early warning flags for Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) and Alzheimer's risk while strictly safeguarding privacy and preventing digital alienation.
- **Recommended Tech Stack**: OpenAI Whisper / ElevenLabs voice cloning and emotional prosody analysis, Fast RAG Memory Stream (LangGraph), longitudinal cognitive health models, React Native / Web Audio API.

#### Proposal 2: Radical Creativity AI Engine: Cross-Disciplinary Concept Synthesizer
- **Problem Statement**: Groundbreaking technological and scientific breakthroughs often emerge at the fringe intersections of distant disciplines (e.g., computational biology yielding synthetic biology; sculptural kinetics inspiring dynamic architectural structures). However, research scientists and product engineers frequently operate in domain silos, lacking structured pathways to cross-pollinate concepts.
- **Task Objective**: Grounded in Aalto's "Radical Creativity" doctrine, teams will build an **interdisciplinary inspiration leap and conceptual hybridization engine** for inventors and researchers. Given an engineering challenge (e.g., "designing wear-resistant biomimetic joints for robotics"), the system projects vector embeddings across orthogonal knowledge bases (e.g., deep-sea cephalopod biomechanics, traditional Nordic birch bark weave structures, ancient Roman mortise-and-tenon masonry), returning imaginative yet physically viable concept drafts and cross-disciplinary architecture blueprints.
- **Recommended Tech Stack**: Graph RAG (Neo4j + LangChain), Multimodal Vision-Language Models (Flux / SDXL), arXiv & Europe PMC academic knowledge graphs, Next.js.

#### Proposal 3: Quantum Sandbox: Hybrid Classical-Quantum Algorithm Prototyper
- **Problem Statement**: Quantum computing is transitioning from pure theory into noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) application. However, software engineers encountering Qiskit or Cirq often find superposition states, multi-qubit entanglement, and unitary gate rotations excessively abstract, hindered by a lack of intuitive visual debugging tools.
- **Task Objective**: Tapping into the Finnish quantum ecosystem (anchored by the Helmi superconducting quantum computer), build a browser-based **hybrid classical-quantum algorithm design workbench and interactive state vector visualization sandbox**. Participants can assemble quantum circuits via visual node graphs or lightweight Python scripts, with real-time Bloch Sphere rendering and entanglement graph topologies, offering one-click simulation for Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithms (QAOA) and Variational Quantum Eigensolvers (VQE).
- **Recommended Tech Stack**: Qiskit / PennyLane, Three.js 3D quantum state visualization, Python FastAPI, WebAssembly.

#### Proposal 4: Circular Bio-Materials Life-Cycle & Performance Predictor
- **Problem Statement**: Aalto University stands at the global frontier of bio-based polymers, nanocellulose, and circular textile fibers (e.g., Ioncell). Yet industrial packaging and consumer goods manufacturers hesitate to substitute conventional petrochemical plastics or synthetic polyesters due to uncertain mechanical degradation, moisture sensitivity, and lack of standardized lifecycle telemetry.
- **Task Objective**: Design an AI-driven predictive platform integrating materials science property databases and cheminformatics representations. R&D engineers input target mechanical constraints (tensile yield strength, impact modulus, targeted ambient biodegradation period), and the model formulates optimal lignin/cellulose/nanocellulose composite recipes while dynamically modeling the cradle-to-grave lifecycle carbon abatement (LCA).
- **Recommended Tech Stack**: Scikit-learn / XGBoost, RDKit materials molecular descriptor extraction, Plotly analytics dashboards, Streamlit.
