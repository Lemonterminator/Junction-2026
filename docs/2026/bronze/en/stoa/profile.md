## 2. Core Products & Technical Architecture

Stoa operates on a three-tier architectural framework comprising a zero-friction data integration tier, a domain-specific construction knowledge graph, and AI-native operational action hubs:

### 2.1 Integrations & Data Ingestion Layer
- **Zero-Friction Ingestion**: Requires zero disruptive behavioral overhaul or painful data migration. Out of the box, Stoa integrates seamlessly with existing AEC software ecosystems (Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud / Revit, Dalux, BIM 360, SharePoint, etc.), parsing and ingesting scattered documents into a unified knowledge repository within 2 business days.
- **EU Data Sovereignty & Strict Privacy Guardrails**: Data is strictly hosted, encrypted, and processed within European Union jurisdiction (EU Data Residency). Proprietary customer drawings and architectural intellectual property are never used to train public baseline models. Enterprisewide automated 30-day hard-deletion policies are fully supported upon request.

### 2.2 Construction-Focused Knowledge Graph & Dynamic Linking
- **Semantic Grounding Between Blueprints and Specifications**: Conventional generic LLMs fail to interpret spatial hierarchies, engineering symbology, and geometric tolerances in architectural blueprints. Stoa builds a specialized construction ontology, establishing bidirectional semantic anchors between 2D drafting sheets, 3D BIM elements, technical specifications, and municipal building codes.
- **Context-Aware Blueprint Navigation**: Clicking on a specific Wall Type on an interactive floor plan instantly surfaces cross-referenced architectural detail sheets, fire-rating specs, stud spacing, acoustic damping criteria, and approved design changes in an adjacent contextual inspector—eliminating the need to flip through reams of physical drawings.

### 2.3 AI-Native Chat & Dynamic Change Feed
- **Strictly Grounded, Verifiable Citations**: When field superintendents ask technical questions via a mobile tablet (e.g., "What is the seismic bracing spacing specified for HVAC ducts on Level 3?" or "Where is the revised structural rebar schedule from yesterday's submittal?"), every single response from Stoa cites exact blueprint revisions, RFI tracking numbers, sheet callouts, and direct page-level anchors.
- **Dynamic Change Radar (Change Feed)**: Rather than relying on easily missed email circulars, Stoa continuously runs diff-analysis across uploaded revisions in the background, proactively highlighting: "The structural engineer shifted Column C4; this directly clashes with previously approved MEP cable tray elevations."

### 2.4 Autonomous Workflow Automation & Document Governance
- When discrepancies or variances are flagged on-site, Stoa automatically chains supporting evidentiary documentation to draft formal industrial incident reports with one click. For repetitive QA/QC workflows, autonomous agents can perform pre-flight compliance checks whenever new drawing packages are published.
