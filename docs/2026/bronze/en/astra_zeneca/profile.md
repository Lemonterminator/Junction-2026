## 2. Core Products & Technical Architecture

### 2.1 Five Core Therapeutic Pillars & Blockbuster Portfolio
AstraZeneca maintains a globally recognized prescription medicine and novel therapeutic portfolio organized across five primary disease domains:
1. **Oncology**:
   - Flagship lung cancer targeted therapy **Tagrisso** (osimertinib, the global standard of care in EGFR-mutated NSCLC);
   - Immuno-oncology checkpoint inhibitor **Imfinzi** (durvalumab);
   - First-in-class PARP inhibitor **Lynparza** (olaparib) for homologous recombination repair-deficient cancers;
   - Revolutionary HER2-directed ADC **Enhertu** (trastuzumab deruxtecan, co-developed with Daiichi Sankyo);
   - Next-generation BTK inhibitor **Calquence** (acalabrutinib) for hematologic malignancies.
2. **Cardiovascular, Renal and Metabolism (CVRM)**:
   - SGLT2 inhibitor **Farxiga / Forxiga** (dapagliflozin), demonstrating transformative clinical efficacy across type-2 diabetes, heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF), and chronic kidney disease (CKD).
3. **Respiratory & Immunology (R&I)**:
   - Gold-standard asthma and COPD combination inhaler **Symbicort** (budesonide/formoterol);
   - Precision monoclonal antibody **Fasenra** (benralizumab) for severe eosinophilic asthma;
   - First-in-class anti-TSLP biologic **Tezspire** (tezepelumab).
4. **Rare Disease (Alexion Business Unit)**:
   - Pioneer complement C5 inhibitors **Soliris** (eculizumab) and next-generation long-acting **Ultomiris** (ravulizumab), treating paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria (PNH) and atypical hemolytic uremic syndrome (aHUS).
5. **Vaccines & Immune Therapies (V&I)**:
   - Adenoviral-vectored COVID-19 vaccine Vaxzevria, alongside long-acting antibody **Beyfortus** (nirsevimab) for Respiratory Syncytial Virus (RSV) protection in infants (partnered with Sanofi).

### 2.2 Digital Transformation, HealthTech & Business Operations
AstraZeneca has evolved far beyond traditional wet-lab small molecules and biologics manufacturing, actively pioneering cloud-native architectures, artificial intelligence, and digital health software platforms:
- **Evinova (Independent HealthTech Business)**:
  - Spun out in late 2023 as an independent, wholly-owned health technology business within AstraZeneca, commercializing clinically validated enterprise digital health software to global biopharma sponsors and clinical research organizations (CROs).
  - Delivers the end-to-end **Evinova Unified Trial Solution**, incorporating Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM), Decentralized Clinical Trials (DCT), digital biomarker ingestion, electronic Patient-Reported Outcomes (ePRO), and digital patient engagement apps.
- **AI-Driven Drug Discovery & Clinical Operations**:
  - Strategic engineering partnerships with hyperscalers and compute leaders (AWS, Oracle, NVIDIA) to construct generative AI clinical pipelines.
  - Large language models automate clinical trial protocol generation, reducing protocol authoring timelines from months to days.
  - Real-World Data (RWD) and Electronic Health Records (EHR) generate "Synthetic Control Arms", substantially lowering patient recruitment hurdles in oncology and ultra-rare disease trials.
- **Open Innovation Ecosystem: A.Catalyst Network**:
  - Global network of 20+ health innovation hubs partnering with startups, university hospitals, and governmental health authorities.
  - In the Nordics, AstraZeneca anchors **BioVentureHub** in Gothenburg and the **CleverHealth Network (CHN)** led by Helsinki University Hospital (HUS), collaborating with Aalto University and health-tech startups to build proactive chronic disease screening algorithms.

## 3. Vision and Mission

### 🎯 Purpose & Mission Statement
> **"To push the boundaries of science to deliver life-changing medicines to patients all over the world as one collaborative team."**

### 🌟 Strategic Long-Term Vision
- **Transforming Patient Outcomes**: Driving a fundamental paradigm shift to transform cancer from a fatal diagnosis into a chronic, manageable, or curable condition, aiming to eliminate cancer as a cause of death.
- **Disease Interception & Remission**: Moving from reactive post-symptom treatment to proactive early detection and molecular interception, combining genomics, multi-omic digital biomarkers, and predictive AI to arrest chronic kidney disease, metabolic syndrome, and heart failure before irreversible tissue damage occurs.
- **Ambition Zero Carbon**: Committed to achieving net-zero greenhouse gas emissions from global operations (Scope 1 and 2) by 2026, and delivering a carbon-negative full value chain (Scope 3) by 2030, setting the benchmark for sustainable pharmaceutical supply chains.

1. **We follow the science**: Guided by empirical rigor, peer-reviewed clinical evidence, and transparent medical data.
2. **We put patients first**: Centering commercial and R&D decisions on prolonging overall survival and enhancing quality of life.
3. **We play to win**: Pursuing world-class scientific breakthroughs to address the most urgent unmet medical needs globally.
4. **We do the right thing**: Upholding the highest standards of bioethics, patient privacy, and regulatory integrity.
5. **We are entrepreneurial**: Maintaining agility, championing continuous learning, and embracing cross-disciplinary computational frontiers.

## 5. Role at Junction 2026 & Challenge Tracks Prediction

As a prominent challenge partner at Junction 2026, AstraZeneca's Nordic team in Espoo operates in immediate proximity to Finland's world-class healthcare registry ecosystem (Findata, Finnish Biobanks, and HUS). The challenge problem statements are projected to center on **"How AI and Digital Health can bridge clinical research and everyday patient care to transform decentralized trials, chronic monitoring, and patient self-advocacy."**

### 5.1 Real-World Healthcare Pain Points
1. **Clinical Trial Recruitment Bottlenecks & Patient Drop-Out**: Up to 80% of global clinical trials face enrollment delays. Patients endure cumbersome travel to centralized trial sites, leading to high protocol abandonment and poor adherence without low-burden digital tracking.
2. **Multimodal Health Data Fragmentation**: Real-world biometric signals from consumer wearables (Apple Health, Garmin), electronic health record PDFs, laboratory reports (HL7/FHIR), and daily lifestyle diaries remain siloed, preventing continuous patient risk profiling.
3. **At-Home Toxicity & Deterioration Monitoring**: Patients undergoing targeted oncology infusions or systemic immunotherapy frequently experience acute toxicities (febrile neutropenia, acute cardiorenal drops) at home, often missing the critical window for early clinical intervention.
4. **Translating Complex Medical Jargon for Patients**: Complex Informed Consent Forms (ICFs) and drug safety leaflets are laden with impenetrable clinical terminology, causing severe patient anxiety and low trial comprehension.

### 5.3 Winning Project Inspirations for Hackers
- **Concept 1: Multimodal At-Home Micro-Expression & Acoustic Biomarker Screener**:
  - Capture ambient wheezing, cough frequency, and deep respiration via smartphone microphones, paired with facial micro-expression analysis of fatigue. Delivers daily passive check-ins for severe asthma and heart failure patients, triggering clinical triage when cardiopulmonary stress deviates from baseline.
- **Concept 2: Interactive Gamified Informed Consent (ICF) Navigator**:
  - Transform a dense 40-page clinical trial protocol into an interactive 3D anatomical explainer with situational Q&A. Patients query potential side effects and dosing schedules in conversational natural language, completing verified e-signatures with comprehensive comprehension validation.
- **Concept 3: "Digital Detective" Diagnostic Copilot for Rare Diseases**:
  - Drawing upon epidemiological knowledge graphs and Finnish clinical registries, the copilot supports primary care general practitioners (GPs) evaluating multi-system idiopathic symptoms, inferring probable rare disease candidates (e.g., PNH, aHUS) and outlining diagnostic referral pathways.
