## 2. Core Products, Services & Supply Chain Operating Model

### 2.1 Full-Assortment Foodservice Catalog & Client Verticals
Valio Aimo delivers an integrated, one-stop food procurement depot exceeding 20,000 SKUs across five primary categories:
1. **Fresh Produce & Proteins**: Premium fresh meats, wild-caught Nordic seafood, seasonal local and imported produce, and pre-cut sanitized vegetables catering to both Michelin-starred kitchens and municipal cafeterias.
2. **Dairy & Bakery Ingredients**: Valio’s signature culinary butter, pure milk, professional mozzarella, specialty whipping creams, bakery flour, and frozen par-baked artisan doughs.
3. **Frozen & Convenience Foods**: Flash-frozen Nordic berries, IQF vegetables, appetizers, and ready-to-heat sous-vide components that dramatically compress back-of-house prep times.
4. **Dry Goods, Condiments & Beverages**: Global spices, extra virgin olive oils, culinary stocks, coffee beans, craft beers, and beverage pairings.
5. **Kitchen Non-Food Consumables**: Eco-friendly biodegradable packaging, commercial dishwashing detergents, sanitation chemicals, and professional kitchen apparel.

**Serving four major institutional customer sectors**:
* **HoReCa Sector**: Hotels, fine-dining restaurants, fast-casual chains, independent cafes, and night venues.
* **Public Sector Catering**: Nationwide elementary and secondary schools, daycares, public hospital networks, eldercare facilities, and municipal kitchens.
* **Bakeries & Artisan Workshops**: Independent bakeries, patisseries, and artisan pizzerias.
* **Industrial Food Processing**: Supplying custom whey proteins, industrial-grade cheese blocks, and specialized dairy fractions to food manufacturers.

### 2.2 Unique "Producer + Wholesaler + Nationwide Cold Chain" Model
* **Producer-Wholesaler Synergy**:
  * While traditional wholesalers merely broker third-party goods on thin margins, Valio Aimo operates alongside Valio's world-class domestic manufacturing plants.
  * Sourcing rare global culinary delicacies (e.g., Italian truffles, Iberian ham) while directly supplying fresh dairy from local plants gives Valio Aimo unmatched traceability, cost control, and quality assurance.
* **Nationwide Multi-Temperature Delivery & Cash-and-Carry**:
  * **Tri-Temp Cold-Chain Logistics**: Fleet vehicles feature segregated multi-temperature compartments maintaining refrigerated (+2°C to +6°C), frozen (below -18°C), and ambient dry-cargo zones simultaneously, preserving unbroken temperature curves across sub-zero Nordic winters.
  * **Pikatukku Wholesale Stores**: Large-format Cash & Carry wholesale stores in Helsinki (Sörnäinen) and Vantaa allow chefs and restaurateurs to replenish urgent perishables directly on demand.
* **Modern B2B Ordering Platforms: VALO & tilaus.valioaimo.fi**:
  * Digital web and mobile ordering suite providing live warehouse inventory visibility, personalized contract pricing, one-click reordering from standard pantry lists, and real-time delivery window forecasting.

## 5. Role at Junction 2026 & Challenge Tracks Prediction

Valio Aimo has proven to be an engaging and high-impact challenge partner across Junction events. During the **Junction AI Hackathon 2025**, Valio Aimo posed the core problem of **"Optimized Deliveries & AI Shortage Prediction,"** sparking intense development among top European developer teams (such as Omni-Valio, Helmi, and Zero-Fail).

At **Junction 2026**, with rising supply chain disruptions, extreme weather volatility, and restaurant kitchen labor shortages, Valio Aimo is expected to expand its prompt into an **"End-to-End Intelligent Food Network: From Weather/Menu-Aware Demand Forecasting to Autonomous Tri-Temp Cold-Chain Routing."**

### 5.1 Real-World Business Pain Points
1. **Perishable Shelf-Life & The "Shortage vs. Waste" Tradeoff**: Fresh kitchen staples (fresh milk, salmon, fresh berries) expire within days. Over-ordering leads to severe write-downs, while under-ordering causes signature menu sell-outs.
2. **Dynamic Shocks on Delivery Fleets & Hubs**: Sudden Helsinki blizzards, ferry delays, or massive music festivals cause local demand spikes that invalidate traditional seasonal time-series models.
3. **The Intelligent Ingredient Substitution Challenge**: When a specific culinary whipping cream faces supply shortages, an AI copilot must instantly suggest substitutes matching fat content, whipping volume, flavor profile, and price per liter directly in the B2B checkout flow.
4. **Invisible Food Waste at the Jobsite**: Kitchen operators lack tools to quantify daily food scraps and waste, resulting in disconnected procurement and consumption patterns.

### 5.3 High-Potential Hackathon Project Concepts
* **Concept 1: "Culinary Substitution Engine" (Food Chemistry & Flavor Ontology)**:
  * Build a domain-specific knowledge graph for HoReCa chefs. If a specific melting cheese is out of stock, the engine analyzes fat-to-protein ratio, moisture content, and melting point to suggest the optimal blend, complete with automated oven temperature and baking time adjustment notes for the head chef.
* **Concept 2: "Aimo Vision Guard" (Walk-in Freezer Inspection & Expiry Copilot)**:
  * A lightweight mobile app allowing chefs to complete a 10-second video sweep of the walk-in cooler. A multimodal vision model tallies inventory, flags yellow near-expiry labels, auto-connects to the Valio Aimo API to replenish depleted items, and prompts the kitchen with "Daily Blackboard Specials" designed to deplete expiring ingredients.
* **Concept 3: "Carbon-Aware Cold Route Optimizer"**:
  * For Valio Aimo's biogas and hybrid fleet, model vehicle refrigeration thermal losses (door opening frequencies), payload weight, elevation changes, and headwinds using reinforcement learning to generate the lowest-emission, zero-spoilage multi-stop delivery schedules.
