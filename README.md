# Junction 2026

Hack Junction 2026 challenges intelligence, partner analysis, and historical challenge archive (2017–2025).

---

## 📂 Project Structure

- **`docs/2026/`**:
  - **`bronze/`**: Raw intelligence, multi-modal scraper outputs, and official LinkedIn announcements.
  - **`silver/`**: Cleaned, verified partner profiles and challenge alignments (bilingual English / Chinese).
  - **`gold/`**: Strategic insights and challenge synthesis.
- **`docs/past/`**:
  - Comprehensive historical archive covering 147 challenges across 2017–2025 remapped into track categories (AI Infrastructure, Agentic AI, Data, Hardware, Healthcare, FinTech) in both English and Chinese editions.
